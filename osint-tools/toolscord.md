### What does ToolsCord do?

ToolsCord helps users make sense of Discord’s internal data formats and features. Instead of collecting data, it provides tools that interpret, format, or enhance Discord-related content for easier use and understanding.&#x20;

**The lowdown:** It provides tools for viewing public Discord assets, decoding platform-specific formats, and improving Discord customisation workflows.&#x20;

### How to Use:

**1. Choose a tool such as a snowflake decoder, timestamp generator, or invite previewer, then input the relevant information.** To find a Discord Snowflake you will first need to enable Developer mode on your Discord client.&#x20;

### Use in Reporting

ToolsCord can support reporting and OSINT workflows involving Discord by:

* Decoding Discord snowflake IDs to understand creation timing.
* Verifying and previewing Discord invite links.
* Interpreting timestamps in Discord-native formats.
* Supporting analysis of Discord webhook usage in investigations.
* Assisting with context-building around Discord activity and identifiers.

### Summary

ToolsCord is best used during the enrichment and interpretation stages of an OSINT workflow to decode and add context to Discord-specific data such as IDs, timestamps, invites, and webhooks. It should be used as a support tool rather than a standalone investigative platform.&#x20;

### Ownership

An independent project not affiliated with Discord or Discord Inc.

### Ethical Considerations

* Avoid using Discord identifiers to harass or target individuals.
* Treat decoded timestamps and IDs as contextual, not identifying evidence.
* Respect Discord’s Terms of Service and user privacy expectations.
* Ensure any OSINT use is proportionate and legally compliant.
