### What does SearchShared do?

SearchShared.info is a file-search engine designed to find files hosted on third-party file-sharing services. Historical descriptions of the site state that it searches shared files across services such as MediaFire, RapidShare and other file-hosting platforms, with searches covering categories including documents, images, videos, MP3s and software.

It’s therefore best understood as a file discovery and pivoting tool, rather than a conventional search engine or people-search service.

**The lowdown:** It can help locate files that may otherwise be difficult to find through normal web searching.&#x20;

### Cookies:&#x20;

Google-domain cookies were observed during testing, including SAPISID, SID, SIDCC, SSID, SOCS, SEARCH\_SAMESITE and UULE. These appear to relate to Google authentication, security, consent and search personalisation rather than SearchShared's core functionality.

### Use in Reporting

SearchShared can be useful for:

* Locating publicly indexed files, documents, images, videos and other material relevant to an investigation.
* Providing investigative leads and source material for reports, briefings and analytical assessments.
* Identifying filenames, file types, hosting services and URLs that may support further lines of enquiry.
* Discovering potentially relevant material that may not be readily surfaced through conventional web-search methods.
* Developing investigative hypotheses by identifying shared files, datasets or documents requiring further examination.
* Supporting findings by directing investigators towards underlying files and hosting locations that can then be independently verified and cited.

**Note:** The platform is an indexing/search service, not primarily a document-hosting service. The underlying files are hosted on third-party file-sharing platforms.&#x20;

| **Capabilities**                                                     | **Limitations**                                                                                           |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Searches for files across multiple file-sharing/hosting services.    | The exact hosting services currently indexed are difficult to confirm.                                    |
| Can locate material using filenames, terms or other search strings.  | Results depend on what has been indexed and may be incomplete or outdated.                                |
| Can turn a keyword or filename into a potential source URL.          | Finding a file does not establish who created, uploaded or owns it.                                       |
| Potentially useful for locating older or obscure shared material.    | Results may lead to external services with different reliability, security and access conditions.         |
| Coverage of documents, images, video, audio and software.            | File-sharing results may include copyrighted, malicious, misleading or otherwise inappropriate material.  |

### Summary

SearchShared is best positioned in the discovery and collection stage of the OSINT workflow, particularly when an investigation involves documents, datasets, images, videos or other files. It’s especially useful for finding potentially obscure shared files but should be treated as a file-discovery/pivot tool rather than an authoritative evidence source.

### Ethical Considerations

* Avoid downloading material unnecessarily.
* Treat unexpected downloads as potentially unsafe.
* Do not open suspicious files on a normal operational workstation.
* Verify the provenance and authenticity of files before relying on them.
* Avoid redistributing sensitive material simply because it can be located through a search engine.
