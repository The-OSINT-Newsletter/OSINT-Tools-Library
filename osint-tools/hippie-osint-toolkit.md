### What does Hippie OSINT Toolkit do?

Hippie OSINT Toolkit pulls together multiple lookup and reverse-search functions into one clean interface, including domain, image, username, and social network tools. This saves the need to install all the tools for quick searches.

**The lowdown:** It’s a quick, clean, beginner-friendly tool, but still useful for experienced investigators who want quick pivots without building custom workflows every time.

### Cookies:&#x20;

Cookies are mostly used to support website functionality, user preferences, and analytics across GitHub, Archive.org, and IntelX. They include session management and login status cookies (logged\_in, cpu\_bucket), appearance and timezone preferences (preferred\_color\_mode, tz), a donation/session identifier for Archive.org (donation-identifier), and a visitor uniqueness/analytics cookie for IntelX (sc\_is\_visitor\_unique).&#x20;

### Summary

Hippie OSINT toolkit shines during quick reconnaissance and social media investigations, especially when analysts need to test leads rapidly without logging into multiple services. Whilst it’s not a replacement for dedicated intelligence platforms, it’s an excellent workflow accelerator for journalists, researchers, cyber investigators, and hobbyist OSINT practitioners alike.
