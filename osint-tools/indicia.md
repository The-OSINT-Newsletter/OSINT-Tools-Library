### What does Indicia do?

Indicia is a comprehensive OSINT investigation platform that consolidates dozens of investigative tools, datasets, and intelligence sources into a single interface. Rather than forcing investigators to jump between separate websites and services, it provides one place to query multiple sources, allowing investigators or the built in AI agent to correlate identities.&#x20;

Functions include person lookups, breach databases, username searches, Discord and Roblox intelligence, image geolocation, reverse face search, and network reconnaissance.

**The lowdown:** The platform allows several investigative tasks to be performed from the same environment.&#x20;

**Note:** Indicia itself warns that AI-generated outputs and automated reports can be inaccurate, incomplete or outdated and should be independently verified before being relied upon.&#x20;

### Use in Reporting

Indicia can be used to:

* Identify potential online accounts associated with a username, email address or other identifier.
* Search multiple breach and compromise databases for information relevant to an investigation.
* Investigate digital identities and connections across supported social and online platforms.
* Conduct network reconnaissance, including DNS, WHOIS, SSL, Shodan and exposed-service information.
* Generate investigative leads by bringing results from multiple OSINT sources into a single workspace.
* Corroborate findings obtained from individual OSINT platforms and underlying source databases.
* Investigate Discord and Roblox accounts, including associated profiles and, where available, connected information.
* Conduct image-geolocation or facial-similarity searches where legally and ethically appropriate.
* Document search results, identifiers and source information for subsequent verification and reporting.
* Support investigative triage by quickly determining which leads warrant deeper examination.

**Reporting caveat:** Indicia should generally be treated as an aggregation and discovery layer, rather than the ultimate evidential source.&#x20;

### Summary

Indicia’s strongest value is in investigative triage, discovery and lead generation as users can run an initial identifier through several relevant sources without manually visiting each service. However, important findings should subsequently be verified using the original source wherever possible.

### Ownership

Owned by Nikolaj Vanhoven, based in Virginia, USA.

### Ethical Considerations

* Treat Indicia results as leads or indicators, not definitive proof of identity, association or activity.
* Verify significant findings against the underlying source before relying on them in reporting. Indicia itself warns that automated and AI-generated results can be inaccurate or outdated.&#x20;
* Avoid unnecessarily submitting or recording personal or sensitive information.
* Do not use Indicia results alone to make decisions that could materially affect an individual.
