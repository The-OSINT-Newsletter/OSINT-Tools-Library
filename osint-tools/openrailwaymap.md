### What does OpenRailwayMap do?

OpenRailwayMap provides a detailed visual representation of railway infrastructure worldwide and is particularly useful for identifying and examining railway routes, stations, track characteristics, electrification, gauges, speed limits and other infrastructure information.

**The lowdown:** It’s a railway-focused geospatial OSINT tool, useful for researching and verifying railway routes, infrastructure and locations rather than general geographic mapping.

### Use in Reporting

OpenRailwayMap can be used to:

* Identify railway lines, stations, junctions, bridges and tunnels in a specific geographic area.
* Establish the approximate location and characteristics of railway infrastructure.
* Examine track type, gauge, electrification and maximum-speed information.
* Identify operational, disused, abandoned or historical railway infrastructure where mapped.
* Cross-reference locations identified through satellite imagery, photographs, videos or other OSINT sources.
* Support geolocation and geographic analysis involving railway infrastructure.
* Provide contextual mapping evidence alongside other independently verifiable sources.

The platform has also been cited as a source in formal infrastructure and planning documentation, including a [Finnish Transport Infrastructure Agency report ](https://www.doria.fi/bitstream/handle/10024/193505/vj_2025-84eng_978-952-405-354-9.pdf?sequence=5)and a [UK Planning Inspectorate environmental statement.](https://nsip-documents.planninginspectorate.gov.uk/published-documents/EN010168-000677-6.3%20Appendix%2019-4%20Lime%20Down%20D%20Desk%20Study.pdf)

### Summary

OpenRailwayMap is a useful geospatial OSINT resource for researching railway infrastructure helping identify routes, infrastructure characteristics and locations, particularly when combined with satellite imagery and other geographic sources.&#x20;

It therefore best fits in the analysis and verification stages of the OSINT workflow, however, because much of the underlying data is crowdsourced from OpenStreetMap, important findings should be independently verified.

### Ownership

Community-developed, non-commercial project operated by its developers and contributors. Its data comes primarily from OpenStreetMap.

### Ethical Considerations

* Do not trespass on railway property to verify information.
* Avoid using railway infrastructure information to facilitate harmful or unlawful activity.
* Treat crowdsourced mapping as potentially incomplete or inaccurate.
* Respect OpenStreetMap/OpenRailwayMap licensing and attribution requirements. The OpenRailwayMap project specifically advises users to obey the law, avoid entering railway areas and use publicly available sources.
