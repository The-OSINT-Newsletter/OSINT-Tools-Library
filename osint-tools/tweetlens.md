### What does TweetLens do?

TweetLens provides a browser reader for captured public posts from a selected collection of X accounts. Search covers the available archive rather than all of X. An archive shows its covered date range and capture information; some posts remain readable after the original disappears.

### How to Use:

1. Open the homepage and choose an account from the public collection.
2. Enter distinctive words in the reader search. For example, the VolIsDead archive supports an `AAPL` search.
3. Narrow the search with `AAPL after:2026-10-01 before:2026-10-10`. These are TweetLens date operators.
4. Inspect the original timestamp and any available thread, reply or quoted-post context. Keep the archive URL and access date with your notes.

The [old-tweet search guide](https://tweetlens.com/find-old-tweets/) also explains X advanced search, the Wayback Machine and private use of your own X export.

## Data Processing

### Account Required:

* [ ] Yes
* [x] No

No account is needed for public browsing. Purchased archives require sign-in.

### Cookies:

The privacy policy says a required session cookie is used for sign-in and form protection. It also describes server request logs and Umami analytics, including reader events that can contain search text, account and filter choices. Avoid confidential search terms.

### Use in Reporting

Use the captured record as a lead when you know the likely account or wording. Check the date, source identity and surrounding material before citing it.

| **Capabilities** | **Limitations** |
| ---------------- | --------------- |
| Word and date search within a chosen public archive. | Covers selected accounts and captured posts only. |
| Captured text and available context can remain readable after removal from X. | Cannot recover an arbitrary deleted post without a saved copy. |
| Public collection needs no login or payment. | Replies, quoted posts and media can be missing; absence isn't proof a post never existed. |

### Summary

A discovery tool for searching the available captured account collections. It can help locate an older post, but its coverage gaps require corroboration.

### Ownership

The public privacy policy identifies Michael as the operator of TweetLens. TweetLens is independent of X. The submitter is affiliated with TweetLens.

### Ethical Considerations

* Respect the privacy and rights of people represented in archived posts.
* Access to a captured copy doesn't grant permission to republish it.
* Don't treat a missing post or missing context as proof of what happened.
