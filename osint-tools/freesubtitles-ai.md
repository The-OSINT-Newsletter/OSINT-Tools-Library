### What does FreeSubtitles AI do?

FreeSubtitles AI uses artificial intelligence to automatically transcribe spoken audio from videos and create subtitle files. It can help convert difficult-to-search video and audio content into text that can be analysed, searched, translated, and referenced.

**The lowdown:** It’s particularly useful when investigating video-based sources where key information is hidden within speech rather than visible content.

### Use in Reporting

FreeSubtitles AI can support OSINT reporting by transforming audio-visual sources into text that can be reviewed and analysed. It is especially valuable when working with:

* Social media videos
* Interview recordings
* Public speeches
* Podcasts
* News clips
* Eyewitness footage

It can be particularly valuable when reviewing conflict footage where language barriers are present.&#x20;

**Note:** Generated transcripts should be manually checked against the original recording before publication, as AI transcription can introduce errors.

| **Capabilities**                                                       | **Limitations**                                                                                                  |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Automatically converts audio and video files into written transcripts. | Transcription accuracy can vary depending on audio quality, background noise, accents, and multiple speakers.    |
| Generates subtitles from spoken content with minimal manual effort.    | AI-generated transcripts may contain incorrect words or missing information.                                     |
| Helps researchers search and analyse video content more efficiently.   | Doesn’t verify whether statements made in recordings are true or authentic.                                      |
| Supports faster review of large volumes of audiovisual material.       | Uploaded files may involve privacy or data protection considerations.                                            |
| Can assist with extracting quotes, timestamps, and key statements.     | Limited value for analysing non-verbal information such as visual context, locations, or objects within footage. |

### Summary

FreeSubtitles AI is best positioned in the collection and processing stages of the OSINT workflow. It’s most useful after identifying relevant audio/video sources, helping investigators convert unstructured media into searchable text for deeper analysis.

### Ethical Considerations

* Ensure you have permission to process private recordings.
* Avoid uploading sensitive or confidential material without appropriate safeguards.
* Treat AI-generated transcripts as a draft requiring human verification.
* Preserve original recordings to maintain evidential integrity.
* Consider whether automated transcription could misrepresent speakers through errors or missing context.
