### What does Predicta Search do?

Predicta Search is a reverse-lookup OSINT tool that helps investigators search and correlate digital identifiers such as email addresses, phone numbers, usernames, and names across publicly accessible online sources. It’s designed to speed up identity discovery, account correlation, and digital footprint investigations by centralising multiple search workflows into one platform.&#x20;

**The lowdown:** It’s essentially a reverse-search engine for online identities.

### How to Use:

**1. Sign in or create an account (for best results). It’s likely you’ll also need to buy some credits for use.**

**2. Enter a target identifier (email, phone number, name or username) into the search bar, and run the search.**

### Use in Reporting

Common use cases for Predicata search include:

* **Fraud investigations:** linking suspicious emails or phone numbers from scam attempts to known online profiles or reused usernames.
* **OSINT research & journalism:** mapping a public figure, source, or alias across multiple platforms to verify identity consistency.
* **Brand protection & impersonation cases:** identifying accounts that are impersonating a company or employee using similar usernames or contact details.
* **Cybersecurity triage:** enriching indicators of compromise (like emails or handles) during incident response to understand attacker infrastructure or reused identities.
* **Due diligence checks:** quickly validating whether contact details provided by a vendor, freelancer, or applicant appear legitimate or are tied to inconsistent identities online.

| **Capabilities**                                                               | **Limitations**                                 |

### Summary

Predicta Search is particularly valuable during early-stage reconnaissance and attribution work, where investigators need to quickly identify whether a target identifier appears elsewhere online. For investigators working in fraud, cyber investigations, due diligence, social media intelligence, or online harms, it can be a highly effective workflow tool.

### Ethical Considerations

* Only investigate publicly available information.
* Avoid harassment or intrusive targeting.
* Verify findings before making attribution claims.
* Use responsibly within legal and organisational guidelines.
* Be cautious when investigating private individuals or vulnerable persons.
