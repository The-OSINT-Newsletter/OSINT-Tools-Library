### What does SPOT do?

SPOT helps users geolocate images by using artificial intelligence to analyse visual clues. It can suggest likely locations, highlight similarities to known places, and provide a starting point for deeper OSINT investigation. It’s particularly useful when dealing with unfamiliar environments or large volumes of images.

**The lowdown:** It excels at speeding up the early stages of analysis but must be used alongside traditional OSINT methods to produce reliable, defensible findings.

### Use in Reporting

SPOT is useful for:

* Quickly generating location leads from images during early-stage investigations.
* Narrowing down possible geographic areas before manual verification.
* Supporting image-based geolocation workflows alongside mapping tools like Google Earth.
* Adding visual context to reports where location is uncertain or disputed.
* Assisting triage of large image datasets by prioritising likely matches.

Whilst there’s currently no direct attribution of the tool in published cases, its functionality mirrors techniques already proven in high-profile OSINT investigations. SPOT just automates part of the process. E.g. we could see its use in cases such as helping geolocate conflict footage and verify events.

| **Capabilities**                                             | **Limitations**                                               |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Quick AI-driven image analysis.                              | Accuracy is inconsistent and results are not definitive.      |
| Identification of visual patterns and geographic indicators. | May generate false positives or overly broad suggestions.     |
| Suggests possible locations to investigate further.          | Highly dependent on search query and available training data. |
| Reduces time spent on initial geolocation triage.            | Cannot interpret context as reliably as a human analyst.      |
| Useful when dealing with unknown or foreign locations.       | Requires manual validation and follow-up investigation.       |
| Can assist less experienced analysts in spotting leads.      | <p><br></p>                                                   |

### Summary

SPOT sits primarily in the analysis and enrichment stage of the OSINT workflow and should be used as a lead-generation tool for image geolocation, rather than a verification tool.&#x20;

### Ownership

### Ethical Considerations

* Misidentification of locations can lead to false accusations or misinformation.
* Be mindful that images may contain sensitive or personally identifiable information.
* AI outputs should be treated critically and not blindly trusted.
* Ensure all findings are verified and legally obtained.
