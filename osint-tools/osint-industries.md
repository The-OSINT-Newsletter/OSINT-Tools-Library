### What does OSINT Industries do?

OSINT Industries provides a real‑time OSINT platform that scans open web sources on demand, avoiding static databases, to return relevant intelligence about a subject’s public digital presence. It supports multiple investigative use cases including law enforcement, cybersecurity, journalism, AML, and fraud detection.

**The lowdown:** It enriches simple identifiers into comprehensive digital footprints and provides tools to visualise and export findings.

OSINT Industries uses a mix of cookies for analytics (Google Analytics), advertising and tracking (Facebook, Google Ads, Reddit, Twitter), and security/session management (Cloudflare and CSRF tokens).

### Use in Reporting

Investigators can leverage OSINT Industries to:

* Verify reported email or phone identity associations.
* Discover linked social accounts and digital traces.
* Extract timeline and location data where available.
* Produce structured reports with export functionality for inclusion in published work.

OSINT Industries has been used by law enforcement to identify suspects and offenders, including tracking fugitive criminals and sex offenders, and supporting child exploitation investigations. View our case studies and investigations [here.](https://www.osint.industries/case-studies)

### Summary

OSINT Industries performs real‑time searches on publicly available digital data to support investigations across law enforcement, cybersecurity, journalism, and other sectors, best used in the analysis and enrichment stage of the OSINT workflow. As always, results should be independently verified.

### Ownership

OSINT Industries is owned and led by British CEO [Nathaniel Fried.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nathanielfried/)

### Ethical Considerations

* Only publicly available information should be used to respect privacy and legal constraints.
* OSINT tools must be used lawfully. Misuse can infringe privacy laws.
* Verification of data sources is critical to avoid publishing unverified personal data.
