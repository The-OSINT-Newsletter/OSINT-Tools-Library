### What does Nuwber do?

Nuwber is a people-search and data-broker service that aggregates information associated with individuals. Searches can be initiated using identifiers such as a name, telephone number, address, or email address.&#x20;

It can help connect identifiers and build an initial picture of an individual by surfacing associated addresses, telephone numbers, names of relatives and other information.&#x20;

**The lowdown:** It aggregates hard-to-manually-compile personal data into a single, searchable interface.

### Use in Reporting

Nuwber can be used to:

* Identify additional addresses, telephone numbers or other identifiers associated with a person.
* Develop leads when investigating an individual, organisation or unknown identifier.
* Cross-reference names, locations and contact details found through other OSINT sources.
* Corroborate information obtained from public records, social media or other people-search services.
* Document profile URLs, associated identifiers and the date information was observed.
* Support identity-resolution and entity-linking during an OSINT investigation.
* Inform further research by identifying additional identifiers that can be investigated independently.

**Reporting caveat:** Results should be presented as leads or corroborating information, rather than definitive proof of identity. Its data can contain errors, dated records, or incorrect associations.

### Summary

Nuwber fits best in the enrichment, entity-resolution, and verification stages of the OSINT workflow. Investigators can use it after identifying a person, telephone number, email address or address to discover additional identifiers and build connections between records.

It’s particularly useful for generating leads and corroborating information from other sources, but should not be treated as an authoritative identity source.

### Ownership

Nuwber Inc. is a US-registered corporation and data broker. Dimitri Shelest is the founder and has [publicly acknowledged retaining an ownership stake](https://krebsonsecurity.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Dimitri-Shelest-Letter-_-Comment-on-Krebs-article-3.20.24-1.pdf) in Nuwber. Tatiana Borodina is [listed as CEO.](https://www.bbb.org/us/va/alexandria/profile/publishers-directory/nuwber-inc-0241-236019003/addressId/259779)

### Ethical Considerations

* Treat Nuwber results as leads, not proof of identity.
* Verify important information using independent sources.
* Avoid unnecessarily collecting or recording personal information.
* Do not use Nuwber information alone to make decisions that could materially affect an individual.
* Exercise additional care when information concerns vulnerable people, protected individuals or sensitive personal circumstances.
