### What does 4plebs do?

4plebs provides an archive and search facility for 4chan content. It preserves historical posts and threads from selected boards, allowing you to find material that may have been pruned or removed from the live 4chan site.&#x20;

**The lowdown:** It’s a historical archive rather than a conventional search engine or social-media platform.

**Note:** An archived post demonstrates that the material was archived by 4plebs but does not, by itself, establish the poster's real-world identity, authorship, intentions or the truth of claims contained within the post.&#x20;

### Use in Reporting

4plebs can be used to:

* Locate and review historical 4chan posts and threads.
* Recover archived material that is no longer available on the live 4chan website.
* Establish the chronology and context of online discussions.
* Corroborate findings identified through other OSINT sources.
* Document archived URLs, board names, thread/post numbers, timestamps and relevant content as evidence.
* Support investigations into online communities, campaigns, narratives and extremist activity.
* Preserve a record of publicly available material for subsequent verification and reporting.

The platform has been used as a research and archival source in journalism/verification work and academic research. [A peer-reviewed study](https://preprints.apsanet.org/engage/api-gateway/apsa/assets/orp/resource/item/60dca4ef7d46cdd303fac999/original/group-salience-inflammatory-rhetoric-and-the-persistence-of-hate-against-religious-minorities.pdf), for example, used data extracted from the 4plebs /pol/ archive, while other academic work has used 4plebs data to analyse online discourse.&#x20;

| **Capabilities**                                                 | **Limitations**                                                                  |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Searches archived 4chan content.                                 | Only covers selected 4chan boards and periods.                                   |
| Can help recover posts no longer visible on 4chan.               | Archived material may be incomplete or unavailable.                              |
| Useful for chronology, thread reconstruction and corroboration.  | An archived post does not prove the identity of its author.                      |
| Can support preservation and documentation of online material.   | Posts can contain misinformation, trolling or deliberately misleading material.  |
| Useful alongside other OSINT sources for verification.           | Archive completeness, media retention and availability can change over time.     |

### Summary

4plebs fits best during the discovery, collection, and verification stages of the OSINT workflow where investigators can use it to locate and preserve historical 4chan posts and threads, particularly where material is no longer available on the live platform. It can then support verification by providing timestamps, thread context and archived content for comparison with other sources.

However, 4plebs is an archive rather than an authoritative source and archived posts should be corroborated independently.

### Ownership

Not publicly disclosed. It’s listed as a ‘community-run archive’.

### Ethical Considerations

* Treat archived posts as public-source material, not proof of identity or authorship.
* Avoid unnecessarily collecting or reproducing personal information.
* Take particular care with extremist, abusive or otherwise harmful content.
* Preserve sufficient context to avoid misrepresenting an archived post or discussion.
