### What does Ground News do?

Ground News collects news articles from thousands of publications worldwide and groups reports covering the same event or subject into individual story pages. This allows users to compare headlines, summaries and coverage from outlets with different editorial perspectives.

**Note:** It does not publish its own original news reporting or act as a conventional fact-checking organisation. Its purpose is to help readers compare existing coverage and understand the media landscape surrounding a story.

**The lowdown:** It’s most useful for those who want to understand how an event is being reported across different parts of the media.

### Cookies:&#x20;

Ground News uses a first-party user\_personalization cookie to remember whether users have viewed its homepage banner and onboarding dialogue. Third-party Microsoft Bing cookies (SRCHHPGUSR, SRCHUID, SRCHUSR and USRLOC) were also observed, supporting search preferences, browser recognition and location-based personalisation. Google cookies (SSID and UULE) were also present during browsing and are associated with Google security and location-context functions.&#x20;

### Use in Reporting

Ground News is useful for:

* Comparing how different news outlets cover the same event or story.
* Identifying stories receiving extensive coverage and those receiving relatively little attention.
* Examining differences in reporting across left-leaning, centre and right-leaning news sources.
* Finding original articles and tracing a developing story back to its initial reporting.
* Comparing coverage from news organisations in different countries and regions.
* Identifying potential gaps in reporting that may warrant further investigation.
* Examining the ownership and factuality ratings of news outlets when assessing sources.
* Building a timeline of how coverage of an event develops across different publications.
* Identifying stories for further fact-checking by comparing the claims and evidence presented by different outlets.
* Supporting balanced media reviews and research into news coverage, political framing and media representation.

| **Capabilities**                                                                                        | **Limitations**                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Aggregates reporting from more than 50,000 international news sources.                                  | Coverage is dependent on the publications and sources indexed by the platform. Not every relevant report will necessarily appear.  |
| Groups articles about the same event to make cross-publication comparisons easier.                      | Articles may be grouped imperfectly, and different reports may contain important differences in scope or context.                  |
| Provides political-bias ratings using three independent monitoring organisations.                       | Bias ratings apply to publications, not individual articles, and the methodology is largely framed around the US political system. |
| Provides factuality ratings and media-ownership classifications for thousands of outlets.               | Ratings are not a substitute for checking an article's evidence, and ownership information may be incomplete or change over time.  |
| Highlights coverage gaps through its Blindspot Feed and provides geographical and coverage information. | A story's absence from one side's coverage does not establish deliberate suppression, censorship or political coordination.        |

### Summary

Ground News is a useful news-monitoring and media-comparison platform that brings together reporting from a large number of international publications and provides tools for comparing coverage, examining media bias, reviewing source ownership and identifying stories that may be underreported.

It’s best used in the discovery and analysis stages of the OSINT workflow with its Blindspot Feed and coverage-comparison features are particularly useful for discovering alternative perspectives and developing investigative leads. However, its ratings apply to publications rather than individual articles, and the platform is not a fact-checking service.

### Ownership

Owned and operated by Snapwise Inc., a Canadian company based in Kitchener, Ontario, operating under the Ground News name.

The company was founded in 2017 by siblings Harleen Kaur and Sukh Singh, who remain its co-founders. Kaur is the company's CEO and Singh is its CTO.

### Ethical Considerations

* Treat differences in media coverage as starting points for investigation, not proof of deliberate bias or suppression.
* Remember that the number of articles covering a story does not establish whether the reporting is accurate.
* Check whether multiple articles rely on the same news agency or original source before treating them as independent corroboration.
* Do not assume an outlet's political-bias rating applies to every article it publishes.
* Consider that bias ratings may reflect US political perspectives and may not translate directly to other countries.
* Be cautious when interpreting stories labelled as underreported, as differences in coverage may reflect geography, editorial priorities or news availability.
