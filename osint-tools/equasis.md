### What does Equasis do?

Equasis is an online maritime research platform designed to promote maritime safety and help reduce substandard shipping. It brings together information from existing databases and information systems so that users can consult relevant ship and company records through one website.

Depending on the vessel and available provider data, records can include:

* Vessel names, identifying information and technical characteristics.
* Ownership, management and other company relationships.
* Classification-society information.
* Port-state-control inspections and recorded deficiencies.
* Insurance and Protection and Indemnity (P\&I) information.
* Other safety-related records supplied by participating maritime authorities and industry organisations.

**The lowdown:** It’s essentially a maritime due-diligence and vessel-research tool.

### Use in Reporting

Equasis can support reporting and investigations by helping researchers:

* Identify a vessel using its IMO number and other recorded characteristics.
* Examine available ownership, management and company relationships.
* Review recorded port-state-control inspections and deficiencies.
* Check the classification information supplied by relevant providers.
* Identify recorded insurance or P\&I-related information where available.
* Compare vessel and company records with information from other maritime databases.
* Identify discrepancies that warrant further research.
* Establish leads for investigating a vessel involved in a maritime incident, alleged illicit activity or a shipping-related dispute.
* Provide documented context for reporting on maritime safety, shipping practices and company networks.

As a real-world example, [a Forbes contributor examined Equasis inspection records](https://www.forbes.com/sites/nishandegnarain/2020/11/25/96-safety-flaws-with-mauritius-oil-spill-ship-wakashio-identified-by-eu-database/) after a bulk carrier, Wakashio, grounded off Mauritius and caused a major oil spill. The article reported numerous recorded deficiencies and discussed questions about the vessel's condition and safety before the incident.&#x20;

### Summary

Equasis is a free, account-based maritime OSINT tool that consolidates ship and shipping-company information from public authorities and industry organisations. It fits best in the collection and acquisition stage of the OSINT workflow, with its main strengths being the breadth of information brought together in one place and the ability to trace records to their providers.

As always, important findings should be corroborated with original provider records or other authoritative maritime sources.

**Important reporting caveat:** A recorded inspection deficiency, company relationship or insurance entry does not automatically demonstrate that a vessel or organisation has acted unlawfully.

### Ownership

Equasis is a publicly funded maritime information project. France and the European Commission were its founding members, and participating maritime administrations subsequently joined through a Memorandum of Understanding.

The website states that it is developed and hosted by the French ministry responsible for maritime affairs.

### Ethical Considerations

* Distinguish recorded vessel or company information from independently verified facts. Equasis aggregates data supplied by other providers.
* Verify important ownership, management and company relationships against the relevant original records, particularly where names are similar or corporate structures are complex.
* Do not infer criminal activity, sanctions evasion, fraud or other wrongdoing from an inspection deficiency, company relationship or insurance record alone.
* Avoid treating the absence of a record as proof that an inspection, relationship or other event did not occur.
* Clearly distinguish historical records from current information, and disclose material uncertainties or discrepancies in published reporting.
* Follow Equasis's registration conditions and data-use restrictions.
