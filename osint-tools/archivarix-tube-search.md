### What does Archivarix Tube Search do?

Archivarix Tube Search helps investigators locate historical copies of videos, channels and metadata that may no longer be available on YouTube.

The tool is particularly useful when videos have been deleted, made private, restricted or removed for policy reasons. Depending on what’s been archived, you may be able to recover video pages, titles, descriptions, thumbnails, channel information and, in some cases, playable video content.

**The lowdown:** The tool doesn't store YouTube videos themselves, it searches publicly available web archives to recover information about videos that are no longer available on YouTube.

### Use in Reporting

Archivarix Tube Search helps journalists and researchers recover and verify YouTube content that has been deleted, made private, or region-blocked.&#x20;

Typical reporting uses:&#x20;

* Verifying what was actually said in a video that has since been removed (via archived subtitles and metadata).
* Reconstructing the publication history of a channel that was terminated or wiped its content.
* Documenting narrative changes when videos are quietly deleted after public attention.
* Retrieving original titles, descriptions.
* Uploading dates and view counts of removed videos for citation in investigations of disinformation, extremism, scams, or public statements.

| **Capabilities**                                                     | **Limitations**                                                             |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Searches 1.5B+ YouTube video records archived since 2005.            | Only finds video content that has been archived and is publicly accessible. |
| Recovered historical metadata including titles and descriptions.     | Archived videos are not always playable.                                    |
| Supports timeline analysis of YouTube activity.                      | Metadata may be incomplete or missing.                                      |
| Provides an easier interface for searching archived YouTube records. | Search coverage is not comprehensive across all YouTube content.            |

### Summary

Archivarix Tube Search is most useful in the discovery and verification stages of the OSINT workflow: establishing that a video existed, what it contained, when it was published, and what was said in it — with archived sources that can be cited as evidence.&#x20;

**Note:** Findings should be corroborated against the original archive snapshots, which the tool links directly.

### Ethical Considerations

* Some videos are deleted for legitimate reasons (privacy takedowns, consent withdrawal, safety of persons shown).
* Consider potential harm before republishing recovered content or metadata.
* Recognise that archived content may not represent the final version published on YouTube.
* Avoid assuming the absence of archived content means it never existed.
