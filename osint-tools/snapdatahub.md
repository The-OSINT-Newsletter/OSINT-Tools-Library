# SnapDataHub

| **SnapDataHub**  | **Quick Overview**                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| URL              | [https://snapdatahub.com/](https://snapdatahub.com/)                                                                                                                                              |
| What it does     | Shows public stats for Snapchat creator accounts (subscribers, Spotlight videos and views, engagement) and how the subscriber count changed since tracking began, without logging in to Snapchat. |
| How to use it    | Open a creator's profile page by username, or browse Rising Creators, then review results.                                                                                                        |
| Cost             | Partially Free.                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Account required | No but the homepage lookup asks for an email.                                                                                                                                                     |
| Cookies          | Request security, login/session management, Google consent and advertising preferences, and live-chat support cookies.                                                                            |
| Ownership        | SnapDataHub ([snapdatahub.com](http://snapdatahub.com)). Its methodology is maintained by Mark Jones.                                                                                             |
| Use in Reporting | Investigating public Snapchat profiles, analysing spotlight reach, comparing creator engagement, tracing subscriber changes, and identifying content trends.                                      |

### What does SnapDataHub do?

SnapDataHub's profile page shows the subscriber count, number of Spotlight videos, total and average Spotlight views, engagement rate, the time of the latest Spotlight post and the date of its last lookup, plus a subscriber-history line (first and latest recorded count, number of readings) for public creator accounts.

The Rising Creators page lists, daily, the accounts that gained the most subscribers recently, with handles and counts. Country pages list the biggest creators per country.

**The lowdown:** It gathers publicly visible Snapchat profile information and Spotlight-post metrics, then organises them into profile reports, searchable creator listings and downloadable datasets.

### Cookies:&#x20;

The site uses a csrftoken cookie for likely request-forgery protection and an sdh\_sid cookie whose precise purpose is undocumented but may relate to application session management. Google-related cookies (FCCDCF and FCNEC) support consent-management and advertising-related functions. Tawk.to cookies (TawkConnectionTime, twk\_idm\_key and twk\_uuid\_\*) support live-chat connection management and returning-visitor recognition.&#x20;

### Use in Reporting

SnapDataHub can be useful for:

* Investigating publicly visible Snapchat accounts and recording their available profile details.
* Examining public Spotlight posts and documenting their views, likes, comments, shares and reposts.
* Tracking historical subscriber counts and identifying periods of notable growth or decline.
* Comparing the performance of creators involved in a public campaign or news event.
* Examining hashtag use, sounds, topics and posting patterns across public Spotlight content.
* Identifying unusually high-performing posts for further investigation.
* Comparing engagement metrics with similar-sized accounts rather than relying solely on follower or subscriber totals.
* Building datasets for quantitative analysis using downloadable CSV files.
* Identifying publicly listed contact channels for legitimate journalistic or research enquiries.
* Providing historical context for reporting on creator influence, online trends and influencer marketing.
* Generating leads for follow-up research using original posts, archived pages and independent sources.

**Reporting Caveat:** A change in subscriber count or a high engagement rate does not by itself prove manipulation, coordinated behaviour or artificial amplification. Earnings estimates should never be presented as confirmed income.

| **Capabilities**                                                                              | **Limitations**                                                                                               |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Public profile stats: subscribers, Spotlight count, total and average views, engagement rate. | Covers public creator accounts only; private accounts and hidden subscriber counts are not available.         |
| 'Stats as of' timestamp on every profile.                                                     | History starts when SnapDataHub first recorded the account, and readings are irregular (days to weeks apart). |
| Subscriber history since tracking began (first and latest count, number of readings).         | The profile page shows the first and latest readings; the full dated series is not printed on the page.       |
| Daily Rising Creators list with handles and subscriber counts.                                | Counts are Snapchat's public totals: they cannot tell real from fake subscribers or explain a change.         |
| Country pages of the biggest creators.                                                        | Influencer search results and CSV downloads are paid.                                                         |
| No Snapchat login needed.                                                                     | <p><br></p>                                                                                                   |

### Summary

SnapDataHub is a free, no-login way to size up a public Snapchat creator and see how their subscriber count has moved, which fills a gap as tools like Social Blade have no Snapchat pages.&#x20;

Its main value is the combination of public-post metrics and dated historical snapshots, which can support trend analysis and investigative leads. Its paid exports are also useful for structured research, but all findings should be checked against original Snapchat content, with collection dates, data gaps and estimation assumptions clearly documented.

### Ownership

SnapDataHub ([snapdatahub.com](http://snapdatahub.com)). Its [methodology is maintained by Mark Jones](https://snapdatahub.com/methodology/), identified as SnapDataHub editor, and the SnapDataHub team. The public pages reviewed do not clearly establish a separate registered corporate owner.

### Ethical Considerations

* Report pages concern real people; a count change says nothing about motive, so avoid implying one.
* A change that coincides with an event is not proof the event caused it.
* Use it for public, mostly large accounts; don't use follower data to target private individuals.
