### What does Osintly do?

Osintly is a unified OSINT platform that returns enriched and correlated results in real time. Instead of juggling a dozen separate tools, you search across pseudonyms, emails, phone numbers, IPs, domains, and crypto addresses from a single workspace and get enriched, correlated results in real time.&#x20;

The platform also includes monitoring, an AI Analyst, investigation project management, team collaboration, and export features. According to the developer, Osintly is the first platform to have an individual module execution page for free: [https://osint.ly/modules](https://osint.ly/modules)&#x20;

**The lowdown:** It’s a powerful all‑in‑one OSINT workbench, great for investigators who want to stop bouncing between tools and start seeing relationships, alerts, and insights in one place.

### Cost

* [ ] Free
* [x] Partially Free
* [ ] Paid

Individual module searches are free to execute. The all-in-one unified platform is paid, with three subscription tiers: Standard at €9.99/month (25 credits), Pro at €24.99/month (100 credits), and Advanced at €94.99/month (200 credits). A 2-day free trial is available for the Pro plan (which we took advantage of).&#x20;

## Data Processing

### Account Required:

* [x] Yes
* [x] No

No to run individual module searches. Yes for full access.

### Cookies:&#x20;

Cookies handle login/authentication, track user activity and analytics (Google Analytics & PostHog), and store session data. [You can view Osintly’s privacy policy here.](https://osint.ly/privacy)

### Use in Reporting

Osintly is ideal for gathering clean, correlated evidence on people or digital entities and presenting it as downloadable documents for reports or briefings.&#x20;

It’s useful to:

* Back up identity verification with correlated data.
* Build timelines and link graphs for investigations.
* Export findings as neat, shareable docs or data files.

Common use cases can be found [here.](https://docs.osint.ly/product/personas)

| **Capabilities**                          | **Limitations**                             |

| One-input multi-data search.              | Data dependent on available public sources. |

### Summary

Osintly is best used in the discovery and enrichment stage to quickly gather, connect, and expand data from a single starting point (like an email or name) but as always, make sure to corroborate your findings.&#x20;
