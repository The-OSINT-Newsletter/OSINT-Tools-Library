### What does Search Systems do?

Search Systems acts as a searchable index of official government databases, directing users to courts, county recorders, property assessors, company registries, licensing authorities, inmate databases and many other public record sources.

The platform covers all 50 US states, over 3,000 counties, and numerous international jurisdictions. It categorises thousands of official databases by subject and geography, making it easier for investigators to locate the correct authority without manually searching multiple government websites.

**The lowdown:** The platform doesn’t host the underlying data, it simply links users to the originating source, acting as a search directory.

### Use in Reporting

Typical reporting uses include:

* Locating court records for civil or criminal proceedings.
* Finding business registration and corporate filing databases.
* Identifying property ownership and land records.
* Accessing professional licensing and regulatory registers.
* Finding inmate, sex offender, or warrant databases where publicly available.
* Supporting due diligence, litigation research, financial investigations and background reporting by directing investigators to primary source records.

You can view examples of where Search Systems has been cited by major media, government agencies, and academic institutions [here.](https://www.searchsystems.net/press)

| **Capabilities**                                                                | **Limitations**                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Indexes thousands of official public records databases in one place.            | Doesn’t host or verify the underlying records.                                                    |
| Covers federal, state, country, and international public record sources.        | Coverage is strongest for the U.S. International coverage is more limited.                        |
| Organises records by jurisdiction and subject area.                             | Linked government databases vary considerably in quality, availability, and search functionality. |
| Links directly to official government databases rather than third-party copies. | Some official sources charge fees or require registration to access records.                      |
| Great for discovering obscure local record repositories.                        | Broken links may occasionally occur as government websites change.                                |

### Summary

Search Systems is best used during the source discovery and record acquisition stages of the OSINT workflow, particularly useful before conducting detailed court, business, property or regulatory record searches.

### Ownership

Search Systems is operated by Search Systems SR LLC, a US-based company. The directory was founded in 1997 and has long been recognised as one of the largest directories of public records resources on the internet.

### Ethical Considerations

* Use records only for lawful and legitimate investigative purposes.
* Respect applicable privacy laws and jurisdictional restrictions.
* Verify information obtained from public records before publication.
* Be aware that public records may contain outdated or incomplete information.
* Do not use Search Systems for decisions regulated by the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA), such as employment or tenant screening.
* Consider the public interest and proportionality when reporting on individuals identified through public records.
