### What does SynapsInt?

SynapsInt enables you to search a wide range of digital identifiers through a single interface. Rather than maintaining its own proprietary database, it queries numerous publicly available sources and presents the results in a consolidated format.

Searches can include email addresses, usernames, domains, IP addresses, phone numbers, cryptocurrency wallets, social media accounts and organisations. Depending on the identifier, results may include associated infrastructure, publicly available account information, technical records and links to external sources.

**The lowdown:** The platform helps accelerate the collection phase of an investigation by reducing the need to search multiple OSINT tools individually.

### Use in Reporting

SynapsInt is useful for:

* Identifying digital footprints.
* Discovering linked online accounts and infrastructure.
* Supporting attribution investigations.
* Generating investigative leads.
* Corroborating findings from other OSINT tools.
* Mapping relationships between digital identifiers.

Whilst there are currently no publicly documented investigations that specifically cite SynapsInt, the platform is often referenced within the OSINT community as an aggregation and pivoting tool for digital footprint analysis.&#x20;

| **Capabilities**                                                       | **Limitations**                                                             |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Searches multiple identifier types from one interface.                 | Results depend on the availability and quality of third-party data sources. |
| Aggregates results from numerous public OSINT sources.                 | Information may be incomplete, outdated, or unavailable.                    |
| Identifies relationships between digital assets and online identities. | Aggregated findings do not confirm identity or ownership.                   |
| Saves time by reducing the need for multiple individual searches.      | Some external data sources may change or become unavailable.                |
| Useful for investigative pivoting and infrastructure discovery.        | False positives are possible and require verification.                      |

### Summary

SynapsInt speeds up the discovery process and provides valuable investigative pivots, enabling investigators to identify new leads, uncover relationships between digital assets, and expand investigations across multiple identifiers.

### Ethical Considerations

* Verify significant findings using independent and authoritative sources.
* Avoid treating aggregated results as definitive evidence of ownership or attribution.
* Clearly distinguish between observed data and analytical conclusions.
* Respect privacy legislation and organisational policies.
