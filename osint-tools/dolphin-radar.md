### What does Dolphin Radar do?

Dolphin Radar is an automated social media analysis platform that focuses on Instagram. Instead of manually checking profiles, scrolling through posts, and calculating engagement rates, the tool processes publicly available data and presents it in a structured, easy-to-read format.

It helps quickly understand how an account is performing and evolving including follower growth trends, engagement patterns, and potential anomalies that might indicate artificial amplification or coordinated activity.

In OSINT workflows, it’s particularly valuable for speeding up repetitive tasks and turning raw social media data into usable insights.

**The lowdown:** It’s a time-saving social media OSINT tool that automates Instagram analysis, turning manual profile checks into fast, actionable insights.

### Cookies:&#x20;

Cookies are mainly used for authentication, session management, and analytics tracking. They include Google-based login tokens and user session data, along with analytics cookies (Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity) to track site usage and performance.

### Use in Reporting

Dolphin Radar is useful for:

* Tracking influencer or public figure engagement trends.
* Identifying fake or suspicious follower growth.
* Supporting social media OSINT investigations.
* Monitoring digital influence and audience patterns.
* Adding evidence to reporting on online behaviour.

In real world terms, Dolphin Radar can be used in investigations to quickly analyse Instagram accounts for signs of fake followers, suspicious growth, or coordinated activity. It helps journalists and analysts back up reporting on influence, scams, or manipulation with data-driven insights.&#x20;

It can also help reveal links between accounts by highlighting shared growth patterns, engagement behaviour, and activity timing. In investigations, this can point to networks of connected or coordinated accounts, rather than isolated profiles.

### Summary

Dolphin Radar is a time-saving analysis tool, sitting in the analysis and enrichment stage of the OSINT workflow after you’ve identified a target. It turns raw Instagram data into quick insights, helping you spot patterns, flag risks, and prioritise deeper investigation.

### Ethical Considerations

* Use only for legitimate analysis of public data.
* Avoid drawing firm conclusions from automated insights alone.
* Be cautious of false positives in “suspicious activity”.
* Respect user privacy and platform terms of service.
