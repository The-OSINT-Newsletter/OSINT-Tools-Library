### What does Social Searcher do?

Social Searcher is a social media search and monitoring platform that indexes publicly available content from multiple social networks. Rather than searching each platform individually, you can perform a single search across supported services to locate public posts, mentions, hashtags, profiles, and discussions.

Depending on the platform being searched, results may include posts, user profiles, timestamps, links, engagement metrics, and associated media.

**The lowdown:** It’s ideal if you need to monitor public conversations, identify emerging topics, investigate online activity, or locate social media evidence.

### Use in Reporting

Social Searcher is valuable for identifying and monitoring publicly available social media activity during investigations or breaking events. It’s most useful to:

* Locate public posts discussing people, organisations, incidents, or events.
* Identify eyewitness accounts and first-hand reporting during developing situations.
* Track hashtags, keywords, and emerging online narratives.
* Discover public profiles associated with usernames or organisations.
* Monitor brand mentions, misinformation, or coordinated online activity.
* Archive and corroborate social media findings before publication.

Organisations like the UK's Open University and Journalism.co.uk have cited Social Searcher as an essential, real-time resource for journalists to find hidden information online and monitor breaking news without needing to log into individual social media platforms. The platform is also frequently used in studies for public commentary and sentiment analysis.&#x20;

### Summary

Social Searcher is most valuable during the collection, monitoring, analysis, and verification stages of the OSINT workflow. Unlike archival tools, it excels at finding and tracking current public conversations.

### Ownership

The platform was founded in 2011 by [Dmitry Oreshko](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dmitrii-oreshko-13b109b1/), a software developer and entrepreneur from Russia, who continues to be publicly associated with the service. It began as a social media search and monitoring platform and has since evolved into a privacy-focused search tool for publicly available social media content.

### Ethical Considerations

* Search and collect only publicly available information.
* Respect platform terms of service and applicable privacy legislation.
* Verify the authenticity of social media posts before using them as evidence.
* Consider the possibility of misinformation, impersonation, parody, or manipulated content.
* Avoid exposing private individuals unnecessarily when reporting findings.
* Preserve evidence by capturing timestamps, URLs, and screenshots where appropriate.
* Corroborate significant findings using multiple independent sources before publication.
