### What does GeoInfer do?

GeoInfer analyses visual elements in a photo, including landmarks, architecture, terrain and vegetation, to estimate where it was taken.&#x20;

It works without EXIF or GPS metadata, so it can help when metadata has been stripped, and returns ranked candidate locations with coordinates and a confidence radius.

**The lowdown:** It’s an AI-assisted image geolocation tool.

### Use in Reporting

GeoInfer can be used to:

* Generate candidate locations for photographs and screenshots that contain no usable GPS or EXIF metadata.
* Assist with geolocating images published on social media, news websites or other public sources.
* Generate leads when verifying the claimed location of an image associated with a news event.
* Help narrow down possible locations of buildings, streets, infrastructure and distinctive landscapes.
* Support investigations involving photographs of possible conflict locations or other geographically significant scenes, where lawful and appropriate.
* Compare an AI-generated location estimate with visible landmarks and independent mapping evidence.
* Identify inconsistencies between a claimed location and the location suggested by the visual features.
* Prioritise further manual research when investigating large collections of images.
* Support image-verification workflows by providing an initial location estimate for independent corroboration.

**Reporting caveat**: Describe GeoInfer's output as an AI-generated location estimate unless the location has been independently established and do not present a predicted coordinate as definitive proof.

### Summary

GeoInfer is an AI-powered OSINT tool, particularly useful when embedded GPS or EXIF metadata is unavailable, and can provide a rapid first-pass location estimate to guide further investigation.

Its most appropriate role is to generate and prioritise location hypotheses.  However, important findings should be checked against independent sources.

### Ownership

GeoInfer is owned and operated by Geoinfer Labs S.L., a company based in Spain.

### Ethical Considerations

* Do not use it to track individuals without consent.
* Be mindful of privacy and sensitive locations.
* Record alternative candidate locations and explain the confidence limitations rather than presenting a single estimate as certain.
* Treat outputs as leads, not facts, and verify before publishing.
