### What does Authentic8 Silo Workspace do?

Silo Workspace provides researchers a secure environment from which to conduct their research. It provides an isolated browser with managed attribution, so the researcher can appear as if they are originating from one of hundreds of locations around the world. This is done through a variety of network connections from Data Centers (similar in use to a VPN), locally purchased ISP connections to increase legitimacy, and multiple different cellular connections.&#x20;

**The lowdown:** Rather than executing web content on the analyst's device, browsing sessions run within Authentic8's cloud infrastructure, reducing exposure to malware, tracking, and attribution risks.

### How to Use:

**1. Download Silo Workspace from the Authentic8 website or use through the web client. After logging in, you can create a new workspace through the Launcher or select from one of your existing workspaces.**

**3. Use built in tools such as:**

* **Collector** – preserves online content as evidence, ensuring it can be reviewed, shared, and reproduced reliably.
* **Case Manager** – organises investigations, evidence, notes, and collected artefacts into structured cases.

### Use in Reporting

Authentic8 Silo Workspace is particularly well suited to threat intelligence teams, law enforcement, journalists, corporate investigators, and due diligence professionals who require secure and attributable online research.&#x20;

Its integrated workflow tools help researchers collect, organise, and export intelligence while reducing operational security risks. Researchers can export screenshots, collected files, browsing artefacts, and case data for inclusion in intelligence reports, investigations, and evidential packages.&#x20;

### Ethical Considerations

* Should be used in accordance with legal and organizational policies; anonymity capabilities could be misused if not properly governed.
* Managed attribution should be used responsibly and not for unauthorised access, fraud, or deception that violates legal or ethical standards.
* Data collection activities should respect privacy regulations and data protection requirements.
* Evidence gathered should be handled according to evidential integrity and chain-of-custody requirements where applicable.
* Investigators should ensure that attribution management features are used for legitimate security, research, journalism, or investigative purposes.
