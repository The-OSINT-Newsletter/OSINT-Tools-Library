### **What does Telegago do?**&#x20;

Telegago enables keyword-based searching across public Telegram content using Google’s indexing system. This can surface posts and channels that may be difficult to find via Telegram’s in-app search, especially when searching across multiple channels simultaneously.

**The lowdown:** The tool functions as a layer on top of Google’s search infrastructure. It does not access private Telegram messages or closed groups and results depend on what Google has indexed, meaning some Telegram content may not appear.

### Cookies:&#x20;

Cookies indicate that Telegago relies on Google’s search infrastructure, including session management, analytics, security verification, and bot protection mechanisms. While suitable for general research, search activity may be logged by Google, sessions may be tied to browser identifiers or IP addresses, and repeated use could potentially be tracked.&#x20;

The takeaway? As always, take appropriate OpSec precautions for sensitive investigations.

### &#x20;Use in Reporting

Telegago is commonly referenced in OSINT communities and is particularly useful for:

* Discovering Telegram channels linked to specific topics
* Tracking misinformation/propaganda campaigns
* Identifying the origin of viral Telegram posts
* Monitoring extremist/conflict-related content
*   Verifying claims by tracing original Telegram posts<br>

    | **Capabilities**                                                                                     | **Limitations**                                                                       |
    | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
    | Uses Google’s advanced search operators and allows phrase searching and filtering.                   | Only indexes publicly available Telegram content and is dependent on Google indexing. |
    | Searches across multiple public Telegram channels simultaneously.                                    | Results may miss newly created or non-indexed channels.                               |
    | Provides access to publicly shared Telegram content without the need to login to a Telegram account. | Cannot access private groups or encrypted chats.                                      |

### Summary

Telegago can support the discovery and situational awareness phase of an OSINT workflow by allowing users to search public Telegram channels and groups by keyword, helping identify relevant posts and trace narratives across multiple channels (without requiring an account.&#x20;

However, it’s limited to content that is both publicly accessible and indexed by Google and does not offer engagement metrics or platform-wide data.

### Ownership

Telegago is a publicly available custom search engine built on Google Custom Search. It is not officially affiliated with Telegram.



### Ethical Considerations:

* Although Telegago only surfaces publicly accessible Telegram content, users should be mindful that ‘public’ doesn’t necessarily mean intended for wider redistribution.
* Searching for and citing extremist, violent or harmful content may unintentionally increase its visibility.
* Always verify surrounding conversation and intent around content (i.e. provide context).&#x20;
* Content found via Telegago should be independently verified. Public Telegram posts can contain misinformation, propaganda, or manipulated media.
* Take care when reporting on or documenting content from private individuals, especially in conflict, protest, or crisis contexts where exposure could cause harm.
* Searching Telegram can expose users to graphic, extremist, or disturbing material. Appropriate safeguarding and support practices should be considered.
