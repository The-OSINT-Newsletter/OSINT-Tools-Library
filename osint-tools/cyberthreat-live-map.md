### What does Cyberthreat Live Map do?

CyberThreat Live Map turns detection data from Kaspersky security technologies into a visual representation of cyber-threat activity. Users can view the globe or a flat map, zoom in and out, change the map's appearance, switch between countries and access additional statistics.

The platform identifies eight detection categories:

* OAS — On-Access Scan: Malware detections when objects are opened, copied, run or saved.
* ODS — On-Demand Scan: Malware detections during a manually initiated scan.
* MAV — Mail Anti-Virus: Detections during scanning of incoming email and related objects.
* WAV — Web Anti-Virus: Detections when web pages are opened or files are downloaded.
* IDS — Intrusion Detection System: Detection of network attacks.
* VUL — Vulnerability Scan: Vulnerability-related detections.
* KAS — Kaspersky Anti-Spam: Suspicious and unwanted email identified through Kaspersky's reputation-filtering technology.
* RMW — Ransomware: Ransomware detection activity.

**The lowdown:** It’s a cybersecurity situational-awareness and visualisation tool that provides an accessible overview of detection activity seen by Kaspersky's technologies.

### Use in Reporting

CyberThreat Live Map can be used to:

* Establish a visual overview of cyber-threat detection activity recorded by Kaspersky technologies at a given point in time.
* Compare reported detection activity across countries and regions using the map and available statistics.
* Examine malware detections across on-access scans, on-demand scans, email and web protection.
* Monitor reported network-intrusion, vulnerability, spam and ransomware detection activity.
* Review live detection rates and historical statistics to identify changes in reported activity over time.
* Identify threat categories or geographic patterns that warrant further investigation.
* Provide contextual background for cybersecurity reporting, threat briefings and OSINT investigations.
* Corroborate potential threat trends against vendor advisories, CISA alerts, malware analyses and independent threat-intelligence reports.

**Important Note:** The map reflects detections recorded by Kaspersky technologies, not all cyberattacks worldwide. Its figures are best used as contextual indicators and investigative leads, rather than proof of a specific incident, attacker, or country's responsibility.

### Summary

Kaspersky CyberThreat Live Map is a free public-facing tool for visualising cyber-threat detections recorded by Kaspersky technologies. It provides global and country views, live statistics, historical summaries and explanations of eight detection categories.

For OSINT research, its main value is situational awareness, background context and lead generation. However, its figures should be treated as vendor-specific detection data rather than a comprehensive or independently verified count of global cyberattacks.

### Ownership

CyberThreat Live Map is operated under the Kaspersky brand. The map's copyright notice attributes the service to AO Kaspersky Lab, a Russian multinational cybersecurity and anti-virus provider company headquartered in Moscow. &#x20;

### Ethical Considerations

* Avoid overclaiming. A map detection does not prove that a system was successfully compromised.
* Country-level data does not establish the nationality, location or identity of an attacker.
* Respect uncertainty by stating that the data represents Kaspersky's observations and detection methods.
* Check the service's terms before republishing screenshots, data or other material.
* Do not use broad detection patterns as a basis for targeting, accusing or exposing individuals without reliable supporting evidence.
