### What does 1stHeadlines do?

1stHeadlines collects headlines from numerous external news organisations and presents them in a centralised interface. The site currently includes categories covering areas such as national, world, business, politics, crime, aviation, military, maritime, technology and other subjects.

**The lowdown:** It’s essentially a searchable news index.&#x20;

**Note:** The site does not primarily produce its own journalism. Its legal disclaimer explains that the headlines are links to stories hosted by the original publishers and that 1stHeadlines' purpose is to provide a convenient central location for viewing them.

### Cookies:&#x20;

1stHeadlines uses third-party advertising technologies, including Google DoubleClick/Google advertising services and Tribal Fusion/Exponential. Observed cookies include IDE, DSID, APC, ar\_debug, mggpc and ANON\_ID. These are primarily associated with advertising delivery, measurement, personalisation and browser recognition rather than the site's core news-aggregation functionality.

### Use in Reporting

1stHeadlines can be used to:

* Identify news coverage of an event, person, organisation or incident.
* Compare reporting across multiple news organisations and identify differences in headlines or framing.
* Establish timelines by recording when different publications reported or updated stories.
* Locate original reporting that can then be reviewed and cited directly.
* Corroborate information found through other OSINT sources by checking whether independent news outlets reported the same event.
* Identify additional sources and leads for further investigation.
* Document headlines, publication names, dates/times and original article URLs as part of an investigative record.

The platform has been documented by academic and information-resource organisations as a news-search and current-awareness resource. For example, the [Internet Scout Report ](https://scout.wisc.edu/report/2000/0428)highlighted its ability to search and monitor headlines across multiple news sources.

| **Capabilities**                                                                | **Limitations**                                                                                        |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Aggregates headlines from a large number of news organisations.                 | It is an aggregator, not the original publisher.                                                       |
| Allows rapid comparison of coverage from different sources.                     | Aggregation does not guarantee that the underlying reporting is accurate.                              |
| Provides topic, geographic and publication-based browsing.                      | Source selection and aggregation methodology are not fully transparent.                                |
| Can help identify original articles and additional investigative leads.         | Headlines can omit important context contained in the underlying article.                              |
| Useful for establishing reporting timelines and monitoring developing stories.  | Older stories may be retained or displayed alongside newer material, requiring careful date checking.  |
| <p><br></p>                                                                     | Links can become outdated or disappear when the original publisher removes or changes an article.      |

### Summary

1stHeadlines’ strongest use in the OSINT workflow is in discovery: finding relevant reporting, identifying additional sources and establishing an initial timeline. It can then support collection by helping investigators locate relevant articles and corroboration by comparing coverage from multiple publishers.

However, it should not normally be treated as the primary source. Once a relevant headline is identified, investigators should follow it to the original publisher, verify the article and cite the original source in the final report.

### Ownership

The specific individual or private corporate parent behind the site has not been publicly disclosed.&#x20;

### Ethical Considerations

* Check the publication date and update time carefully when constructing timelines.
* Compare multiple independent sources rather than assuming multiple headlines represent independent reporting.
* Be aware that several outlets may be repeating the same original report or wire story.
* Preserve the original headline, publisher, timestamp and URL when documenting findings.
* Avoid presenting an aggregated headline without the surrounding context of the original article.
* Consider source reliability and editorial standards when assessing the underlying publication.
* Remember that 1stHeadlines itself states that external sites are outside its control.
