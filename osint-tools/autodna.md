### What does autoDNA do?

autoDNA helps uncover what may be hidden behind a VIN. From accident records and mileage discrepancies to theft alerts and registration history, it's a quick way to build a picture of a vehicle's past and spot potential red flags before digging deeper.&#x20;

**The lowdown:** It’s particularly useful for identifying potential fraud indicators and verifying vehicle-related claims.

### Use in Reporting

autoDNA is most commonly used when:

* Verifying vehicle ownership claims.
* Identifying mileage discrepancies.
* Checking for accident or write-off history/
* Screening for theft records.
* Supporting asset tracing enquiries.
* Validating information found in vehicle advertisements.

Whilst the platform hasn’t been cited in major investigations/reports, it is widely used within vehicle fraud, insurance, due diligence, and OSINT workflows as a vehicle-history verification tool.

**Note:** Information obtained through autoDNA is generally used to support investigative findings rather than serve as a primary evidential source.&#x20;

### Summary

autoDNA is most useful within OSINT, insurance, fraud, and due diligence workflows. It is most commonly used as a supporting source rather than a primary evidential source, helping investigators verify vehicle histories and generate new lines of enquiry.

### Ethical Considerations

* Verify significant findings through independent sources before publication/operational use.
* Be aware that vehicle history records may be incomplete, inaccurate, or outdated depending on the jurisdiction and data provider.
* Avoid making assumptions about individuals based solely on vehicle ownership or vehicle history data.
* Clearly communicate any limitations in the data when citing autoDNA findings in reports.
* Use the tool proportionately and only for legitimate investigative, research, or due diligence purposes.
* Maintain an audit trail of searches and findings where investigations may be subject to review or scrutiny.
