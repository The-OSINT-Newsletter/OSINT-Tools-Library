### What does Socialprofiler do?

Socialprofiler answers two questions from one identifier. First, which public accounts on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and X are the same person: you give it a US phone number, a username, a profile link, or a name and US state, and it resolves candidate accounts across the four networks. Second, what is actually in that public footprint: it reads public follows, likes, hashtags and posts and returns categorised interests (thousands of categories), the mutual-connection graph, location and profession signals, political and financial interest signals, and anything unusual or controversial as flagged interests.

Every report ends with an "Ask anything" chat where you question the same public data in plain language. Results are presented as candidates with the evidence behind them; a human still has to confirm identity.&#x20;

There is a documented REST API ([https://dashboard.socialprofiler.com/docs/](https://dashboard.socialprofiler.com/docs/), and public docs repository at [github.com/twicethinker/socialprofiler-api-docs](https://github.com/twicethinker/socialprofiler-api-docs)) for people who automate collection rather than click through a UI.

**The lowdown:** It’s a US-focused social-media background-check and lead-generation tool.

### Cookies:&#x20;

Cookies identified include a Socialprofiler-domain cookie, spsession, and six cookies associated with Yandex domains: sync\_cookie\_ok, yabs-sid, yandexuid, ymex, yp and yuidss.

The spsession cookie is consistent with first-party session management. The Yandex cookies appear consistent with identifier management, cookie synchronisation, session handling and browser or preference metadata. The yp value contains screen-d.

### Use in Reporting

Socialprofiler can potentially be used to:

* Generate preliminary leads about an individual's publicly visible social-media activity.
* Identify interests and recurring themes suggested by public posts and profile information.
* Locate social-media accounts that may warrant closer manual review.
* Organise publicly available material into a more structured research starting point.
* Identify claims, posts or account associations that need independent verification.
* Provide background leads for further research using original platform content, archived pages and other reliable sources.

**Reporting caveat**: These are potential research applications, not confirmation that every result is accurate or complete. Do not infer criminality, intent, credibility or dangerousness from an AI-generated flag alone.&#x20;

### Summary

Socialprofiler is a commercial social-media background-check tool that aggregates publicly available information and uses AI to produce summaries of a person's interests and potential risk indicators (US subjects only). It may help identify leads for further research, but its outputs require verification against original sources.&#x20;

**Important note:** It produces candidates rather than confirmed identities.

### Ownership

Socialprofiler, Inc., California, USA. Founder-run; US Patent No. 12,517,965.

### Ethical Considerations

* Socialprofiler is not a consumer reporting agency under the FCRA; results must not be used for employment, tenancy, credit or insurance eligibility decisions. It uses public data only.&#x20;
* Treat AI output as lead generation, not proof: confirm identity by opening the accounts.&#x20;
* Consider the subject's privacy and the potential for harm before sharing a report; a person with no public presence will not appear, and anyone can ask for removal via the opt-out page.&#x20;
* The service is restricted to US subjects and is geo-fenced away from EU, EEA, UK and Russian users.
