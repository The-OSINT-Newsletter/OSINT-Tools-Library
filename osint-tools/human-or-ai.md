### What does Human or AI do?

Human or AI is an interactive quiz that helps investigators, researchers, journalists, educators, and OSINT analysts build instinctive recognition skills for AI-generated imagery. It exposes how realistic modern generated faces have become and teaches users to slow down and inspect details critically.&#x20;

**The lowdown:** The more you play, the better you become at spotting subtle anomalies that can indicate a synthetic identity.

### How to Use:

**1. Visit the site and begin the quiz. Each round presents a new face image for analysis.**

<img src="../.gitbook/assets/unknown (144).png" alt="" height="235" width="602">

**2. Investigate the image like an OSINT analyst. Slow down and inspect background details, eye alignment, hair edges, accessories, reflections and shadows etc.** <br>

**3. Select either “Human” or “AI” and review the answer. Pay attention to what fooled you so you can learn from it.**

### Use in Reporting

Rather than being used directly in reporting/investigations, it works well as:

* An OSINT training exercise.
* A misinformation awareness tool.
* A synthetic media detection practice platform.
* A fun team challenge during investigations training.

The tool demonstrates how convincing generated profile imagery has become and why image verification should form part of investigative workflows.&#x20;

| **Capabilities**                                               | **Limitations**                                                    |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Helps users practise identifying AI-generated profile images.  | Not a forensic or definitive AI detection tool.                    |
| Improves awareness of synthetic media and fake personas.       | Does not analyse metadata or image provenance.                     |
| Provides quick, interactive OSINT-style training.              | AI-generated images are becoming harder to distinguish over time.  |
| Encourages attention to visual inconsistencies and anomalies.  | Should not be used alone to verify authenticity in investigations. |

### Summary

Human or AI works best as a practical awareness exercise for investigators, journalists, researchers, students, and security teams looking to strengthen visual verification skills in an age of increasingly realistic AI-generated imagery.

### Ownership

Human or AI is part of [HumanizeAI](https://humanizeai.com/about/), based in Atlanta and “created by a team of Edtech and AI experts focused on educational and ethical AI writing tools.”

### Ethical Considerations

* Avoid treating the tool as definitive proof that an image is fake or authentic.
* AI detection should support, not replace, broader verification methods.
* Be cautious when making public accusations based solely on visual assessment.
* Remember that generated images may be used maliciously in scams, harassment, or disinformation campaigns.
