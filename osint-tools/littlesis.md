### What does LittleSis do?

LittleSis is an open-source database that documents relationships between influential individuals, corporations, politicians, lobbyists, government officials, think tanks, universities, foundations and other institutions, predominantly in the U.S.&#x20;

Rather than acting as a traditional people search engine, it visualises how powerful entities are connected through employment, board memberships, donations, lobbying, ownership, family relationships and other affiliations. The platform combines publicly available information with researcher contributions and cites its sources wherever possible.

**The lowdown:** It’s particularly valuable during the early stages of investigations when mapping networks and identifying potential avenues for further research.

### Use in Reporting

LittleSis is particularly useful during background research and network analysis. It can quickly identify relationships that may not be obvious through conventional searching, helping develop hypotheses, identify conflicts of interest and uncover influence networks.

Examples of its use in reporting include:

* Investigating board interlocks between corporations, charities and universities.
* Identifying political donations and lobbying relationships surrounding legislation.
* Mapping connections between government officials and private industry during procurement investigations.
* Researching corporate ownership and executive networks linked to sanctions, corruption or financial crime.
* Finding common directors or advisors across multiple organisations during company investigations.
* Supporting investigations into elite influence networks, where LittleSis has been referenced by investigative journalists and OSINT practitioners, including guidance published by Bellingcat.

### Summary

LittleSis is best used during the discovery, background research and network-mapping stages of the OSINT workflow. It’s ideal for identifying influential relationships, generating investigative leads and revealing organisational structures that may warrant further examination.

### Ownership

LittleSis is operated by the [Public Accountability Initiative (PAI)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_Accountability_Initiative), an independent, non-profit research organisation based in the United States that investigates corporate and political power structures.&#x20;

### Ethical Considerations

* Treat LittleSis as a secondary research resource rather than definitive evidence.
* Verify all significant findings against primary documentation before publication.
* Avoid inferring misconduct solely from documented relationships or associations.
* Be mindful that network maps illustrate connections, not proof of influence or wrongdoing.
* Ensure reporting provides appropriate context and accurately represents the nature of each relationship.
