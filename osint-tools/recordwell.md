### What does Recordwell do?

Recordwell searches public sources for possible people matches in the United States, starting with a first and last name. It provides search previews and paid full reports. Users can also request removal of their own information for free. (Sensitive breach details are made available only after the owner verifies the email address.)

**The lowdown:** It can help you organise information that would otherwise require multiple searches across public records, news sources and websites.

### Use in Reporting

Recordwell can be useful for:

* Identifying potential public-record matches associated with a person's name and contextual details.
* Locating public web profiles, news mentions and other published references for further investigation.
* Discovering potentially relevant court, legal, property, professional or business records, where available.
* Comparing multiple identifying fields to help distinguish a subject from namesakes.
* Identifying original source pages or issuing authorities that can be consulted for direct verification.
* Supporting preliminary digital-footprint research by organising dispersed information into a structured report.
* Documenting search categories, returned results, unavailable checks and unresolved identity matches.
* Reviewing your own publicly visible information and finding routes to request removal from Recordwell or other publishers.

**Reporting Caveat**: A possible name match is not proof of identity, wrongdoing or a relationship.&#x20;

### Summary

Recordwell is  a name-based public-source research option for preliminary US people-search leads with its most useful OSINT role in initial subject research and lead development.&#x20;

Its limits include paid full reports, possible data errors and namesake ambiguity and it should supplement authoritative verification rather than serve as identity proof or an eligibility screening tool.

### Ethical Considerations

* Use only for lawful, proportionate research.
* Respect privacy and avoid harassment, stalking, discrimination or unlawful surveillance.
* Corroborate possible matches and findings before relying on or publishing them.
* Minimise disclosure of personal information and consider potential harm.
* Do not use reports for employment, tenancy, credit, insurance or other FCRA eligibility decisions.
* Use the free opt-out route only for your own information, following the service verification process.
