### What does Library of Leaks do?

Library of leaks acts like a meta-index for leaked data collections, helping researchers find where certain compromised datasets might be referenced or stored. Instead of hosting the leaks itself, it points you toward known sources across the OSINT ecosystem.

**The lowdown:** It’s basically a search layer over leak ecosystems, however it does not guarantee data accuracy or completeness, and results should always be verified against primary sources.

### Use in Reporting

Library of Leaks is especially useful for:

* Early-stage investigative research.
* Mapping exposure of organisations or domains.
* Finding pointers to larger datasets elsewhere.
* Cross-referencing OSINT leads quickly.

In real-world use, Library of Leaks is a behind-the-scenes OSINT discovery tool used to surface breach leads during early investigation and enrichment, but it's rarely cited directly in public case reports.&#x20;

| **Capabilities**                               | **Limitations**                                            |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fast keyword search across leak references.    | Does not host full datasets.                               |
| Aggregates multiple OSINT leak sources.        | Results may be incomplete or outdated.                     |
| Useful for domain/email reconnaissance.        | Requires verification via original sources.                |
| Helps surface hidden or obscure datasets.      | Can surface sensitive material requiring careful handling. |
| Good starting point for breach investigations. | No guarantee of data authenticity or context.              |

### Summary

Library of Leaks sits mainly in the discovery stage of the OSINT workflow, best used as a starting point to locate potential breach-related material, not as a final authority.

### Ethical Considerations

* Contains references to sensitive and potentially personal data.
* Must only be used for legitimate purposes.
* Do not attempt to access or redistribute leaked personal information.
* Always verify findings through lawful and ethical channels.
* Be mindful of GDPR implications when handling EU-related data.
