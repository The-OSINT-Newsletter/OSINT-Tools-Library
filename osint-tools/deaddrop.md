### What does Deaddrop do?

Deaddrop is a specialist OSINT tool designed to index and search Telegram content, including public channels and groups. Since Telegram’s native search is limited, Deaddrop helps investigators uncover conversations, usernames, and communities that would otherwise be difficult to find.&#x20;

**The lowdown:** It’s particularly useful for tracking activity across niche, hidden, or loosely moderated spaces where subjects may operate.

### How to Use:

### Use in Reporting

Deaddrop can support:

* Tracking narratives, influence campaigns, and disinformation.
* Monitoring entities, keywords, and channels of interest.
* Source discovery and verification for journalists.
* Due diligence and open-source background research.
* Real-time incident monitoring and threat intelligence.

While no public investigations explicitly attribute findings to Deaddrop, numerous real-world OSINT cases demonstrate the importance of Telegram intelligence gathering. Tools like Deaddrop support the discovery of Telegram channels and communities, which is a critical early stage in investigations involving fraud, extremism, and organised crime.

### Summary

Deaddrop is a focused and valuable Telegram OSINT search tool that helps uncover hidden or hard-to-find communities, best used as a discovery and pivot tool rather than a complete investigative solution.
