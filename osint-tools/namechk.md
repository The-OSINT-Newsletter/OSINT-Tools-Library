### What does Namechk do?

Namechk is an online username and domain-name availability checker. It allows you to enter a name or handle and check whether it is available across a large selection of social networks, online platforms, and domain extensions.

The platform advertises checks across more than 90 social-media accounts and 30 domain options. Its listed platforms include Facebook, X (Twitter), YouTube, Twitch, Tumblr, TikTok, WordPress, eBay, Yelp, Flickr and PayPal.

For OSINT purposes, Namechk can be used to investigate a known username or alias. If a handle is shown as unavailable on a particular platform, this may indicate that the name is already registered or reserved there. You can then follow the platform link and check whether a relevant public profile exists.

**The lowdown:** It’s an availability checker rather than a comprehensive account-discovery or identity-verification service.

### How to Use:

**1. Enter the username you want to investigate into the search field. Complete the CAPTCHA if prompted and run the search.**

**2. Review the results across the listed social networks, online platforms, and domain extensions. Note which platforms show the username as unavailable and which indicate that it is available.**
