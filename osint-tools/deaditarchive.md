### What does deaditArchive do?

deaditArchive is an independent archive preserving a selection of posts from Reddit communities that have been removed or purged from Reddit.

The site describes itself as “reviving a glimpse” of purged communities and currently provides browsable archives of numerous communities. These include communities such as consumeproduct, The\_Donald, ChapoTrapHouse, CringeAnarchy, Wojak and YoungPeopleHateSpeech.

**The lowdown:** It can be useful when material has disappeared from Reddit but an archived copy remains available.

### Use in Reporting

deaditArchive can be used to:

* Recover historical posts from purged Reddit communities.
* Investigate how online communities developed over time.
* Verify claims about previously deleted Reddit content.
* Trace historical narratives and recurring themes.
* Identify material for further source investigation.

As an example, if you were investigating the history of an online political movement, you could use deaditArchive to recover posts from a subreddit that has since been banned or removed. You could then identify dates, usernames, recurring narratives and links, and cross-check those findings against archived webpages, contemporary news coverage and other social-media sources.

### Summary

deaditArchive is best used within historical research and content recovery, particularly when investigating communities or discussions that have disappeared from Reddit. It can provide valuable leads for researching online communities, historical narratives, controversial movements and deleted discussions.&#x20;

However, investigators should not assume that the archive is complete or that every archived post is an exact representation of the original Reddit page.

### Ownership

There is limited publicly visible information on the site's ownership or archival methodology. The site identifies itself as deaditArchive and credits 'make\_something' as its creator. It links to communities.win and is hosted at deaditarchive.netlify.app.

### Ethical Considerations

* Verify archived material before treating it as authentic.
* Preserve context when quoting deleted or controversial posts.
* Avoid presenting archived claims as established facts.
* Consider the privacy implications of republishing deleted material.
* Take extra care with extremist, hateful or abusive content.
* Clearly distinguish archived material from current statements.
