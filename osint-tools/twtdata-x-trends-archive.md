| URL                          | [https://twtdata.com/twitter-trends/](https://twtdata.com/twitter-trends/)                                                                                                        |
| What it does                 | Shows which topics and hashtags were on X's (Twitter's) trending list in a given country or city on a past date so you can check when and where a topic trended.                  |
| How to use it                | Pick a country or city, then a date (or edit the date in the URL), and review results.                                                                                            |

### What does twtData X Trends Archive do?

twtData collects and displays trending topics from X across around 30 geographic locations ((including the United States, United Kingdom, Germany, France, Brazil, India and Japan) It keeps one page per location per day listing the topics that were on X's trending list there. The list is updated hourly and each trend shows its best position and how many hours it stayed on the list, plus a 0-100 'Score' that is twtData's own measure, not a tweet count.&#x20;

Each topic also has its own page listing the locations it trended in, when it was first and last seen, and its best rank. Older days show the topics and their Score but no best position or hours. Tweet volumes are not shown.

**The lowdown:** It can help identify topics worth investigating, establish a preliminary timeline, and direct researchers to related public X content.

### Cookies:&#x20;

The site sets first-party session cookies, including twd\_sid, and uses Tawk.to live-chat cookies such as TawkConnectionTime, twk\_idm\_key and twk\_uuid\_\* to support chat connections and visitor continuity. Google cookies were also observed, including SOCS, which stores cookie-consent preferences, and SSID, associated with Google services.

### Use in Reporting

twtData X Trends Archive can be useful for:

* Identifying trending hashtags, topics and keywords on X (formerly Twitter) across worldwide and country-specific trend lists.
* Reviewing historical trend data to establish when a topic appeared and how its ranking or duration changed over time.
* Comparing the geographic spread of a trend across different countries or locations.
* Identifying recurring topics and hashtags that may warrant further investigation.
* Developing leads related to breaking news, public events, online discussions or emerging narratives.
* Locating relevant X pages and public posts linked to archived trend entries for further examination.
* Documenting trend rankings, recorded dates, duration, geographic coverage and the archive's displayed metrics.
* Supporting timeline analysis by comparing trend activity with independently verified events, news reports and other open-source information.
* Recording changes in a topic's trend-list presence while distinguishing the site's proprietary trend score from actual post volume or public engagement.

| **Capabilities**                                                                                | **Limitations**                                                                                 |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Daily trending lists per country and city; the United States goes back to at least August 2023. | Not a live feed of tweets: it shows trending lists only, no tweet content and no tweet volumes. |
| About 30 locations update hourly, with each trend's best position and hours on the list.        | Older days show no best position or hours on the list.                                          |
| Topic pages: every location a topic trended in, first and last seen, best rank.                 | Most cities have one update a day; hourly detail exists only for about 30 locations.            |
| No account and no X login needed.                                                               | Trending lists are X's own algorithmic output, which can be pushed by coordinated campaigns.    |
| Plain URLs per location and date, easy to cite and archive.                                     | Gaps are possible on days when no list was captured.                                            |

### Summary

twtData provides a quick, free way to answer 'was this trending on X, where and when?' without an X account or API access. It’s best for the timeline and verification stages of an investigation, however keep in mind it covers trending lists only (no tweets, no volumes) and that older days carry no position data.

### Ownership

Operated by twtData ([twtdata.com](http://twtdata.com)). Nothing further disclosed.

### Ethical Considerations

* Trending lists are aggregated and contain no personal data, but topics can name private people caught up in a news story; take care when quoting them.
* A topic trending is not proof of organic interest: bots and coordinated campaigns can push topics onto the list.
* Hashtags may be ironic, multilingual, ambiguous or used by different communities for different reasons. Make sure to review the underlying posts and their surrounding context.
* Make sure to corroborate with other sources before saying why something trended.
