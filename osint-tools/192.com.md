### **What does 192.com do?**

192.com acts as a people-finding and address lookup tool for the UK. It allows users to look up individuals, addresses, businesses, and property information using publicly available data sources such as the electoral roll, phone directories, and company records.

The platform includes information such as:

* Names and addresses from the Open Electoral Register
* Historical address data
* Business listings and director records
* Phone directory listings
* Property price and ownership information

**The lowdown:** 192.com is essentially the online equivalent of a UK phone book combined with electoral roll data. However, because it relies heavily on the Open Electoral Register, it only includes people who have not opted out of having their details shared publicly.

### Use in Reporting

192.com can be useful for:

* Verifying where someone lives or has lived.
* Identifying individuals connected to an address.
* Cross-checking contact information.
* Finding links between people and businesses.
* Background research for journalism or investigations.

As an example, following the UK data-privacy investigation known as ‘Operation Motorman’, reporting revealed how journalists sometimes used databases and investigators to obtain personal information for stories. Some industry sources noted that reporters could often obtain publicly available contact information simply through directory services like 192.com or traditional directory enquiries.

### Summary

192.com combines electoral roll data, phone directories, company records, and property information into a single searchable platform. While it can be very useful for investigations and background research, users should be aware that many results require payment and not all individuals will appear in the database.

### Ownership

Owned by 192.com Ltd, a private limited company founded in 1997 and based in London, by entrepreneur [Alastair Crawford](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alastaircrawford/?originalSubdomain=uk), who remains the majority shareholder.
