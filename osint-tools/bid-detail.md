### What does Bid Detail do?

Bid Detail aggregates publicly available procurement data from government tender portals into a searchable platform. This means rather than searching multiple procurement websites individually, you can quickly locate contracts, suppliers, buyers and spending information in one place.

The platform provides insight into procurement activity over time, allowing analysts to identify recurring suppliers, contract values, purchasing trends and links between organisations.

**The lowdown:** Because the info originates from official procurement notices, it’s an efficient way to access contract data without manually searching multiple government procurement portals.<br>

### Use in Reporting

Bid Detail can support reporting by:

* Verifying public contract awards.
* Demonstrating procurement relationships.
* Identifying supplier histories.
* Quantifying public spending.
* Supporting financial and corporate investigations.
* Providing evidence of commercial links between organisations.

| **Capabilities**                                                           | **Limitations**                                                                          |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Quickly searches thousands of public procurement notices in one interface. | Only covers publicly available procurement information.                                  |
| Identifies government contract winners and purchasing organisations.       | Advanced searching and full datasets require a paid subscription.                        |
| Reveals contract values, award dates, and procurement history.             | Coverage varies depending on the procurement authority and published data.               |
| Builds supplier profiles showing public sector customers over time.        | Historic records may be incomplete where source data has changed/been removed.           |
| <p><br></p>                                                                | Procurement awards don’t necessarily indicate current or ongoing business relationships. |

### Summary

Bid Detail fits best during the enrichment and relationship-mapping stages of an OSINT investigation. It’s particularly effective when building organisational profiles, tracing commercial relationships, investigating public spending or identifying links between suppliers and public bodies.

### Ethical Considerations

* Procurement data should be interpreted within its proper context.
* Winning a public contract does not imply wrongdoing or preferential treatment.
* Verify significant findings against original procurement notices wherever possible.
* Consider the publication date and current status of contracts before drawing conclusions.
* Present procurement information accurately and avoid implying relationships that are not supported by the available evidence.
