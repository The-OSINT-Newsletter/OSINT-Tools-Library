### What does Etherscan do?

Etherscan is a blockchain explorer that allows you to search and investigate activity recorded on Ethereum and supported EVM-compatible networks. It can be used to examine wallet addresses, transactions, token transfers, smart contracts and blockchain activity.&#x20;

For OSINT investigations, the platform is particularly useful when a cryptocurrency wallet address or transaction hash is already known and further on-chain investigation is required.&#x20;

**The lowdown:** It turns blockchain data into a searchable and readable format.

Cookies observed include Google Analytics (\_gid), an ASP.NET session identifier, Cloudflare security (cf\_clearance) and Etherscan preference/localisation cookies. ### Use in Reporting

Etherscan can be used to:

* Trace cryptocurrency transactions between wallet addresses.
* Investigate wallets linked to scams, fraud or other suspicious activity.
* Identify patterns of cryptocurrency movement and fund flows.
* Corroborate findings from other cryptocurrency and OSINT sources.
* Document transaction hashes, wallet addresses, timestamps and amounts as evidence.
* Support investigations into cryptocurrency-related activity and financial crime.
* Inform risk assessments when profiling wallets, transactions or organisations.<br>

| **Capabilities**                                            | **Limitations**                                               |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Search wallet addresses, transactions, and smart contracts. | Wallet addresses don’t directly identify individuals.         |
| Trace cryptocurrency and token transfers.                   | Transaction activity doesn’t prove intent or criminality.     |
| View wallet balances and transactions histories.            | Etherscan labels may require independent verification.        |
| Investigate token and contract activity.                    | Some activity may involve exchanges or intermediary wallets.  |
| Identify links between wallet addresses.                    | On-chain data provides limited off-chain context.             |
| Record transaction hashes for verification and reporting.   | <p><br></p>                                                   |

### Summary

Etherscan is best used during the discovery, verification and evidence-gathering stages of the OSINT workflow where cryptocurrency or blockchain activity is relevant.

It should primarily be treated as a source of on-chain evidence rather than identity attribution. A wallet address can be followed through the blockchain, but connecting that address to a real-world individual or organisation generally requires additional evidence from other OSINT sources.

### Ownership

Etherscan is an independent, privately held company owned and led by its founder and CEO, [Matthew Tan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/matthew-tan-4a3021a7/). He launched the platform in 2015, and it’s headquartered in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia.

### Ethical Considerations

* Use blockchain data for legitimate investigative purposes.
* Do not assume a wallet address identifies a specific person.
* Verify Etherscan labels with additional evidence.
* Avoid presenting investigative assumptions as confirmed attribution.
* Consider privacy when reporting wallet activity.
* Take care when handling potentially sensitive financial information.
* Follow applicable laws, policies and organisational procedures.
* Do not connect your own wallet to services unnecessarily during passive research.
