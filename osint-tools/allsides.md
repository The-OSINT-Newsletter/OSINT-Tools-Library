### What does AllSides do?

AllSides provides tools intended to help people understand how political perspectives influence news coverage and how different outlets report on the same subjects.

Its main functions include:

* Media Bias Ratings: Rates news outlets and some writers as Left, Lean Left, Center, Lean Right, Right or Mixed, depending on the type of source.
* Balanced News: Presents stories from sources with different political leanings so readers can compare their coverage.
* Bias Checker: Helps users assess the political leaning of a news article.
* AllStances: Provides a way to explore differing perspectives on important issues.
* Media literacy resources: Explains common forms of media bias, including omission, sensationalism, slant, framing and word choice.
* Research and educational services: Offers resources and services for schools, organisations, newsrooms and other professional users.

The platform states it has rated more than 2,400 media sources. Its ratings are based on a combination of methods, rather than solely on the opinions of website visitors.

**The lowdown:** It’s a media-bias analysis and cross-perspective news comparison tool.

### Use in Reporting

AllSides can be used to:

* Compare how news outlets with different political leanings report on the same event or issue.
* Identify differences in headlines, framing, terminology, emphasis and story selection across news sources.
* Find alternative reporting and perspectives that may reveal additional details or investigative leads.
* Examine how political bias may influence the presentation of news and current affairs.
* Identify potential gaps, omissions or inconsistencies between reports covering the same subject.
* Support investigations into political communication, disinformation narratives and media coverage of controversial events.
* Build a more diverse set of news sources for researching elections, conflicts, protests and political developments.
* Provide context for analysing how different media organisations portray political figures, policies and public events.
* Support fact-checking by helping researchers locate alternative coverage and claims that can be verified against primary sources.

**Important note:** AllSides' media bias ratings indicate political perspective, not factual reliability. Any claims or apparent inconsistencies identified through comparison should be independently verified before being presented as findings.&#x20;

### Summary

AllSides is a media literacy and news comparison platform that can help you understand political bias and compare how different outlets cover the same events. For OSINT, its main value is helping investigators discover alternative reporting, examine differences in narrative framing, and identify potential gaps for further investigation. It’s best used during Collection and Acquisition, with a secondary role in analysis and processing.

However, its ratings should be used as a guide to source perspective, not as a substitute for checking the evidence in individual articles. Important findings should also be corroborated using primary records and independent sources.

### Ownership

AllSides Technologies, Inc. operates AllSides as a US public benefit corporation. Its stated mission is to help people understand different political perspectives and reduce the effects of information bubbles.

The company was co-founded by John Gable, Scott McDonald and Joan Blades. According to its published ownership information, the founders maintained majority ownership and control as of November 2023; an equity crowdfunding campaign in 2024 broadened its ownership.

### Ethical Considerations

* Do not equate political leaning with dishonesty. A source's bias rating does not prove that its reporting is false.
* Avoid treating the centre as automatically correct. A centre-rated outlet can omit important perspectives or publish inaccurate reporting.
* Account for changing ratings. Record the rating and the date checked, because AllSides may revise its assessments.
* Examine the methodology. Note whether a rating is based on a blind survey, editorial review, independent review or a combination of methods.
* Distinguish omission from deception. A story may leave out a detail because of space, editorial priorities or incomplete information; omission alone does not prove deliberate manipulation.
* Avoid false balance. Presenting multiple perspectives does not mean that all claims have equal evidential support.
