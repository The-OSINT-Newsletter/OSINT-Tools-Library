# TK Metrics

| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| URL              | [https://tkmetrics.com/](https://tkmetrics.com/)                                                                                                                                                                              |
| What it does     | Helps size up a public TikTok account (real reach per video, not just follower count) and test claims about what it earns, using dated stats reports and a free money calculator that applies TikTok's Creator Rewards rules. |
| How to use it    | Open a creator report, or type a username into the money calculator, then review results.                                                                                                                                     |

### What does TK Metrics do?

TKMetrics publishes dated stats reports for 1,349 public TikTok accounts (e.g. [https://tkmetrics.com/blog/khaby-lame-tiktok-profile-stats/](https://tkmetrics.com/blog/khaby-lame-tiktok-profile-stats/),data as of 25 Jun 2026). Each report covers followers, plays, likes, saves and shares per view over a sample of recent videos, posting days and hours, top videos, hashtags and sounds, with the analysis date printed on the page.

A free money calculator ([https://tkmetrics.com/tiktok-money-calculator](https://tkmetrics.com/tiktok-money-calculator)) fills in followers and average views for accounts in its database (or you type them in) and gives a Creator Rewards range and a brand-deal range.&#x20;

Where it holds an account's recent videos, it counts only videos of a minute or more, because TikTok's Creator Rewards program pays only on those.&#x20;

**The lowdown:** Its reports combine public profile information with metrics from approximately 300 recent public videos, making it easier to examine how an account performs over time.<br>

### Cookies:&#x20;

The site uses a csrftoken cookie for request-forgery protection and Google-related cookies (FCCDCF and FCNEC) associated with consent management and advertising-related state. Tawk.to cookies (TawkConnectionTime, twk\_idm\_key and twk\_uuid\_\*) support live-chat connection management and visitor recognition. The first-party tk\_ft cookie contains a signed-looking value, but its precise purpose has not been independently verified.

### Use in Reporting

TK Metrics can support:

* Researching public TikTok creators.
* Recording follower counts and profile details.
* Comparing engagement rates between creators.
* Examining posting frequency and consistency.
* Identifying unusually successful videos.
* Investigating public hashtag use.
* Tracking changes across dated profile snapshots.
* Supporting influencer and campaign investigations.
* Building datasets for further analysis.
* Cross-checking creator performance with original TikTok posts.
* Documenting observation dates for reproducibility.
* Providing contextual performance comparisons within follower tiers.

| **Capabilities**                                                                                                                      | **Limitations**                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Dated stats reports for 1,349 public TikTok accounts: followers, plays, likes, saves and shares per view, engagement rate.            | Reports are snapshots, rebuilt when the account is collected again, not on a schedule; some are months old (the date is on each page).                |
| Money calculator: Creator Rewards range that counts only videos of a minute or more, plus a brand-deal range.                         | Built from a sample of recent videos, not the full history.                                                                                           |

| Creator Rewards eligibility check against TikTok's minimums (10,000 followers, 100,000 views in 30 days, videos of a minute or more). | Accounts outside the database need their numbers typed in.                                                                                            |
| Methodology page explaining samples and formulas.                                                                                     | Public data only: no private accounts, no audience demographics, no deleted videos.                                                                   |
| No account needed for reports or the calculator                                                                                       | Influencer search is limited (three creators, handles hidden) without a paid plan.                                                                    |

### Summary

TK Metrics is a partially free TikTok OSINT and analytics platform that combines public profile information with structured analysis of recent public videos. Its strongest investigative features are engagement analysis, dated profile snapshots, consistency grading and transparent methodology.

It’s particularly valuable for discovery, collection and preliminary analysis and most useful in the verification and account-profiling stage: sizing an account's real reach and checking earnings or popularity claims made about it.&#x20;

**Note:** Significant findings should be verified against original TikTok content, and estimated figures should always be identified as estimates.

### Ownership

TKMetrics. Its [methodology](https://tkmetrics.com/methodology/) is written and maintained by Mark Jones.&#x20;

### Ethical Considerations

* Data is public, but reports aggregate it about identifiable people: share only what the investigation needs.
* Earnings figures are estimates; never present them as an account's actual income.
* Snapshot dates matter: check the analysis date before citing a number, and re-check on the live profile.
* Smaller or private individuals deserve more care than large public creators; avoid amplifying harassment targets.
* Data removal requests can be sent to the site (about page.
