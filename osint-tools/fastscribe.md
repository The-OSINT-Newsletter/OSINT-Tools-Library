# FastScribe

FastScribe turns an audio or video recording into timestamped text. A reporter can locate a quotation in a long recording, then return to the original audio to check the words and context.

### How to use

1. Upload a recording with permission to process it, or sign in and paste a supported public video URL.
2. Open the result and use the timestamps to compare a passage with the original recording. Uploaded files also support speaker labels.
3. Export an editable transcript or download an SRT subtitle file.

### Cost and access

The published free limits include 15 guest credits for an uploaded file. Larger workloads use credits from a paid plan or credit package. A free account is sufficient to submit a supported video link if it has enough credits.

### Use in reporting and limitations

Timestamps help an editor find a passage for review. Speaker labels distinguish turns but do not establish a speaker's identity. Automatic transcription can mishear names or overlapping speech. Confirm quoted words against the recording before publication.

### Data processing and ethical considerations

FastScribe processes media on its servers. Obtain consent where appropriate and check the provider's retention and deletion terms before uploading confidential interviews. The original recording remains the primary source. FastScribe is a hosted service from the FastScribe team. The materials cited below do not identify a legal jurisdiction for the operator.

### Related tools
