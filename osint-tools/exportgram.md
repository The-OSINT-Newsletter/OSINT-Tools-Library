### What does ExportGram do?

ExportGram captures publicly available information from Instagram profiles and converts it into downloadable reports, making it easier to review content offline, preserve evidence, and analyse social media activity without manually collecting screenshots.

The platform helps document posts, captions, hashtags, timestamps, engagement metrics and profile information into excel, CSV and JSON formats.

**The lowdown:** It massively reduces manual effort and preserves evidence that can be referenced during investigations.

### Use in Reporting

ExportGram is useful for:

* Preserving Instagram profiles before content changes or is deleted.
* Archiving posts for evidential purposes.
* Building social media timelines.
* Identifying hashtags, mentions and recurring themes.
* Analysing posting frequency and activity patterns.
* Monitoring public accounts over time.
* Capturing engagement metrics for analysis.

### Summary

ExportGram is a specialist Instagram collection tool that simplifies the capture and preservation of publicly available social media data and best fits during the collection and preservation phases of the OSINT workflow.&#x20;

It’s particularly effective once a relevant Instagram profile has been identified, allowing investigators to capture content before it changes/is deleted. The exported data can then be used during the analysis phase to examine timelines, engagement, hashtags, relationships and behavioural patterns.

### Ethical Considerations

* Collect only information that is publicly available.
* Respect privacy, legal and organisational policies.
* Avoid excessive automated collection that may breach platform terms of service.
* Record collection dates and methodology to maintain transparency.
* Validate exported information against additional independent sources before drawing conclusions.
