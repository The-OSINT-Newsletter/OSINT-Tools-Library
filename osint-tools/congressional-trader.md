### What does Congressional Trader do?

Congressional Trader aggregates public STOCK Act financial disclosure information into searchable pages. It helps investigators, journalists, analysts, and citizens find lawmaker trades by politician, ticker, state, transaction type, and disclosure timing.&#x20;

**The lowdown:** It’s best used as a discovery layer for congressional trading activity, with final facts verified against the underlying public filings.

### Use in Reporting

Congressional Trader can support reporting on:

* Public financial disclosures.
* Congressional trading patterns.
* Sector exposure.
* Ticker-specific lawmaker activity.

It should be treated as a lead-generation and research interface, not the final source of record. Final citations should refer back to official House or Senate disclosure filings when possible.

| **Capabilities**                                          | **Limitations**                                                  |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Search public congressional stock trade disclosures.      | Disclosure data can lag actual trade dates.                      |
| Browse by lawmaker, ticker, state, and recent trades.     | Amounts are usually reported as ranges, not exact values.        |
| Identify disclosure timing and transaction patterns.      | Public filings may contain amendments or corrections.            |
| Compare activity across public officials.                 | The tool should not be treated as investment advice.             |
| Use as a discovery layer for further source verification. | Important findings should be verified against official filings.  |

### Summary

Congressional Trader is most useful in the discovery and triage phase of OSINT or accountability reporting. Its strengths are fast search, public disclosure aggregation, and lawmaker/ticker views. Its main limitation is that public STOCK Act disclosures are delayed and range-based, so findings should be verified against official filings before publication.

### Ownership

Congressional Trader is an independent web tool focused on public U.S. congressional stock trading disclosures. The site presents information derived from public STOCK Act disclosure data and related public sources.

### Ethical Considerations

* Use public disclosure data responsibly.
* Do not imply wrongdoing based only on a reported trade.
* Verify dates, amounts, and identities against official filings.
* Avoid using the tool as investment advice.
* Give context about disclosure delays and range-based reporting.
