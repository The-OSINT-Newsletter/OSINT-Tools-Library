### What does Export Comments do?

Export Comments is a social media data extraction tool that collects comments from public online content and converts them into structured datasets.

Instead of manually copying comments from social media platforms, users can extract hundreds or even thousands of comments from supported posts, videos, or accounts and then export them for analysis.

**The lowdown:** Ultimately the tool automates much of the collection process by turning comment sections into downloadable datasets.

### Summary

For OSINT investigators, journalists, and researchers, Export Comments can significantly reduce the time required to gather audience reactions and preserve discussions for analysis. While it’s most valuable during the collection, archiving and analysis stages of the OSINT workflow, users should remember that exported comments represent only part of the wider conversation and should be interpreted within their broader social and platform context.

### Ownership

Export Comments is a privately held, independent SaaS company. It was founded in 2018 and operates out of the European Union. The company has not raised any external venture funding and its exact parent corporation or single beneficial owner is not publicly disclosed

### Ethical Considerations

* Ensure collection activities comply with applicable laws and platform terms of service.
* Respect privacy expectations, particularly when analysing comments from private or semi-private communities.
* Avoid collecting more personal data than necessary for the investigation.
* Preserve context when reporting on comments to avoid misrepresentation.
* Be aware that comments may include bots, coordinated accounts, satire, or inauthentic behaviour.
* Verify significant findings through additional sources and methods.
* Consider data retention and storage requirements when handling large datasets.
