### What does Facebook Video Downloader do?

Facebook Video Downloader lets you grab copies of publicly accessible Facebook videos and save them locally. As we know, content on Facebook can disappear quickly, and having a preserved copy ensures you can analyse, verify, and reference it later.&#x20;

**The lowdown:** It’s a handy evidence-capture tool for OSINT, but it’s dependent on external reliability.

### How to Use:

**1. Find a public video on Facebook and copy and paste its URL into the Facebook Video Downloader search bar.**

**2. Select your preferred quality (HD/SD), download the video, and save the file.**                                         &#x20;

### Use in Reporting

Downloading and preserving social media video is a core OSINT practice for evidence collection, verification, and reporting.&#x20;

This tool in particular is commonly used to:

* Preserve video evidence before deletion.
* Analyse footage frame-by-frame.
* Archive content for investigations.
* Include verified media in reports.

As an example, groups like Bellingcat regularly preserve videos from platforms like Facebook before they’re removed so they can later be used as evidence in reports or legal contexts.

### Summary

Facebook Video Downloader is a straightforward tool for capturing and preserving Facebook video content. It’s best used in the collection and preservation stage of the OSINT workflow., and whilst it’s not analytical in nature, it is essential for ensuring evidence isn’t lost.

### Ownership

According to [Apple App Store listings from 2025,](https://apps.apple.com/bj/app/fdown-net/id6751503770) Devesh Gehlot is listed as the provider and developer for the fdown.net application. It’s a third-party web tool not affiliated with Facebook.

### Ethical Considerations

* Only download and use publicly available content.
* Respect copyright and platform terms of service.
* Avoid misuse or redistribution without context.
* Ensure proper attribution in reporting.
* Be mindful of privacy, especially with sensitive footage.
