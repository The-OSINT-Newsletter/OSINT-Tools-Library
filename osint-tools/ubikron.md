### What does Ubikron do?

Ubikron automatically captures webpages, extracts entities, preserves evidence, organises research, and provides AI-assisted analysis to help investigators manage complex online investigations. Rather than simply recording where you've been online, the platform actively helps you work with the information you've collected.&#x20;

**The lowdown:** One of its biggest strengths is reducing context switching as it helps centralise investigative workflows directly within the browser.

### How to Use:

**1. Install the Ubikron browser extension, create a new investigation project and conduct online research as normal, ensuring ‘navigation recording’ is turned on.**

### Summary

Ubikron sits between evidence collection tools and analytical platforms, helping investigators preserve, organise, enrich, and analyse information gathered from open sources. It’s particularly useful for analysts managing large or complex investigations where traceability, organisation, and evidence preservation are critical.

### Ownership

Ubikron is developed by Vortimo, an independent South African software company founded by [Roelof Temmingh](https://www.linkedin.com/in/roeloftemmingh/), who previously co-founded both SensePost and Maltego.&#x20;

### Ethical Considerations

* Secure collected evidence appropriately, particularly when using cloud deployments.
* Validate AI-generated outputs before relying on them for decision-making.
* Avoid collecting excessive or unnecessary personal information.
* Consider proportionality and necessity when preserving online content.
* Follow organisational policies governing OSINT investigations and evidence handling.
