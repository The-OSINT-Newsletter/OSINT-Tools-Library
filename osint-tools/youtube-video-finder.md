### What does YouTube Video Finder do?

YouTube Video Finder is an OSINT pivot tool. Instead of searching for videos, it takes a known YouTube URL or video ID and quickly connects you to multiple external platforms. These include YouTube itself, archiving services, and specialised video analysis tools, helping you investigate content more efficiently across the web.&#x20;

**The lowdown:** It’s a fast and practical pivot tool for YouTube investigations and need quick verification. It doesn’t help you find videos but once you have one, it helps you explore it everywhere that matters.

### Use in Reporting

YouTube Video Finder can be used to:

* Cross-check video availability across platforms.
* Locate archived or alternative versions.
* Support verification workflows with multiple sources.

| **Capabilities**                                                                  | **Limitations**                                                                           |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Retrieves data from a known YouTube video.                                        | Cannot search by keywords or discover new videos. (Requires an existing video URL or ID). |
| Works with both URL and video ID.                                                 | Dependent on YouTube data availability.                                                   |
| Integrates with YouTube, Wayback Machine, Distribute YouTube Archive, and Filmot. | Cannot access private or fully removed videos.                                            |
