### What does Fagan Finder do?

Fagan Finder is a collection of search and research tools designed to help find information online. Rather than functioning as one search engine, it acts as a directory and gateway to a large range of search services.

It covers general search engines as well as social media, news, government information, academic research, books, documents, data, libraries, archives, images, video, podcasts and other specialist resources. It also includes resources in multiple languages and countries.

**The lowdown:** The site is specifically designed to help find information that may be difficult to locate through Google alone e.g. info held in specialist databases, behind alternative search interfaces or outside the main search engine index.

### Use in Reporting

Fagan Finder can be used to:

* Find relevant people, organisations, websites and documents.
* Locate specialist databases and less obvious information sources.
* Search international and non English sources.
* Identify news reports, archives, academic material and government records.
* Pivot from an initial lead into related platforms and information sources.
* Locate original sources for verification and evidential reporting.&#x20;

The platform is widely included in OSINT, cybersecurity and investigative research guides as a search aggregation and source discovery tool.

### Summary

Fagan Finder is a search and source discovery hub rather than a conventional search engine with its main value in the breadth of resources it brings together.

It fits best into the discovery and collection stages of the OSINT workflow, with particular value during pivoting. A user can start with a name, organisation, topic, image or keyword, identify relevant specialist resources, and then move between them to expand the investigation.

### Ownership

Owned and managed by its creator, [Michael Fagan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mfagan/), based in Canada.

### Ethical Considerations

• Use information only for legitimate and lawful investigative purposes.

• Treat search results as leads rather than verified facts.

• Verify significant claims using the original source and, where possible, independent sources.

• Consider the privacy implications of searching for or reporting information about individuals.

• Be aware that linked services may collect search data, IP addresses or other information under their own policies.

• Take additional care when accessing sensitive, restricted or dark web resources. Fagan Finder itself warns that dark web content can include illegal and illicit material.

• Minimise the collection and retention of unnecessary personal information.&#x20;
