### What does Search Files do?

Search Files is a specialised search engine designed to locate files hosted on external file-sharing platforms.

Rather than searching only conventional webpages, it focuses on finding files and associated information from services such as MediaFire, MEGA, Keep2Share, Rapidgator, and other file-hosting services referenced by the website.

The website states that it can search for information within files. However, its search results are powered by Google Custom Search, so it should not be assumed that every file is fully indexed or that the contents of every result have been examined.

**The lowdown:** It can be useful when researching material that has been uploaded to file-sharing services rather than published on conventional websites.

### Use in Reporting

Search Files is useful for:

* Finding publicly indexed documents hosted on third-party file-sharing services.
* Discovering reports, presentations and other files associated with a company, organisation or individual.
* Locating publicly available material that may not appear prominently in conventional search results.
* Searching for documents using names, phrases, keywords and date filters.
* Identifying external file-hosting links that may provide leads for further investigation.
* Finding publicly accessible software packages and technical documentation for research purposes.
* Supporting research into the online distribution and publication of documents.
* Identifying potentially relevant files for subsequent verification and analysis.
* Comparing search results across different file-hosting services.
* Recording publicly indexed file links and retrieval dates as part of an investigative research trail.

### Summary

Search Files is a straightforward file-host search engine that can help researchers locate publicly indexed material. Its main value for OSINT is discovery as it can help identify files and documents that may be relevant to an investigation and provide links for further examination.

However, it should be treated as a starting point rather than a verification tool. Search results do not establish the identity of an uploader, the authenticity of a document or the legality of downloading or redistributing it.

### Ownership

Search Files is operated by Elias Lange, based in Hamburg, Germany, according to the website's [Impressum](https://searchfiles.de/impressum.php) and [privacy policy.](https://www.searchfiles.de/datenschutz.php)

The website states that it is not affiliated with Google or the file-hosting services it searches. Its search results are powered by Google Custom Search.

### Ethical Considerations

* Only access and use files for legitimate research and investigative purposes.
* Respect copyright, licensing restrictions and the intellectual property rights of file owners.
* Do not assume that a publicly accessible file is free to download, reproduce, or redistribute.
* Avoid downloading confidential, private or sensitive material without a lawful basis and appropriate justification.
* Do not open suspicious files on an ordinary computer or an unprotected network.
* Remember that a file's presence on a hosting platform does not establish who uploaded it.
* Consider whether publishing a discovered document could expose personal information or put individuals at risk.
