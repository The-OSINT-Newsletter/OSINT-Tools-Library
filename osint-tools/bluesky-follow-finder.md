### What does the Bluesky Follow Finder do?

Bluesky Follow Finder is a simple OSINT discovery tool that highlights accounts a profile is likely to be interested in based on their existing network.

It focuses on overlap, i.e. people who are followed by many accounts a profile already follows, but who that profile hasn’t followed yet.

This makes it useful for quickly identifying:

* Influential voices within a niche
* Emerging accounts in a community
* Shared points of interest across a network

**The lowdown:** It’s great for uncovering popular accounts in a network but only shows connections rather than activity or private info.

### Use in Reporting

Bluesky Follow Finder can support:

* Identifying relevant or influential accounts within a network.
* Discovering community clusters indirectly through shared follows.
* Expanding an investigation outward from a known account.
* Finding additional leads for manual analysis.

While examples of public, high-profile investigations are limited due to the platform’s relative newness, the tool has clear applications in real-world scenarios. Analysts and journalists could use it to identify clusters of accounts, uncover key influencers, track the spread of misinformation, and monitor niche communities or political movements. It could also be useful for threat intelligence, helping researchers detect linked or coordinated accounts.

### Summary

Bluesky Follow Finder is ideal for quick exploration and lead generation, best used in the collection and analysis stages of the OSINT workflow. However, it should only be used as a starting point, not a standalone analysis tool.

### Ownership

### Ethical Considerations

* Use only publicly available data.
* Avoid assuming influence purely from follow overlap.
* Be aware of algorithmic bias toward larger accounts.
* Validate findings with additional sources.
* Respect user privacy and platform norms.
