### What does That's Them do?

That’s Them gathers information from public records, marketing databases, online directories, business registrations, property records, court filings, social media data, and other legally accessible sources. It then returns associated records and linked data points to help build a profile of an individual, including names and aliases, current and historical addresses, phone numbers, email addresses and more.

**The lowdown:** It’s a digital shortcut through thousands of scattered public records, although every result as an investigative lead, not confirmed intelligence (people move house and numbers change!)&#x20;

**Note:** The tool only searches people based in the US.&#x20;

### Use in Reporting

That’s them is useful for:

* Identifying and verifying individuals connected to a story or investigation.
* Discovering contact information for potential interview subjects or witnesses.
* Tracing historical addresses and establishing location timelines.
* Mapping family, household, or associate relationships that may reveal useful connections.
* Generating investigative leads that can be corroborated through public records, social media, court documents, and other OSINT sources.

| **Capabilities**                                                                                | **Limitations**                                                             |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Quick identifies individuals using names, numbers, emails, or physical addresses.               | Primarily focused on US-based individuals & records.                        |
| Performs reverse lookups on numbers, emails, addresses, IP addresses and VINs.                  | Information may be outdated, incomplete, or inaccurate.                     |
| Reveals current & historical addresses associated with a person.                                | Source transparency is limited, making verification difficult.              |
| Identifies potential relatives, associates and household members.                               | Search results can generate false positives, particularly for common names. |
| Aggregates data from multiple public and commercial sources into a single, searchable profile.  | <p><br></p>                                                                 |

### Summary

That's Them is best used as an enrichment and lead-generation tool, rather than a primary source or final verification tool, most useful after you have obtained an initial identifier (such as a name, email address, phone number, or address) and need to expand your understanding of the target.

### Ownership

### Ethical Considerations

* The fact that information is publicly accessible does not automatically make every use of that information ethical.&#x20;
* Have a legitimate purpose for searching.
* Respect privacy and data-protection laws.
* Avoid harassment, doxxing, or intimidation.
* Verify information before acting on it.
* Consider whether disclosure serves a genuine public interest.
* Minimise unnecessary collection or retention of personal information.
