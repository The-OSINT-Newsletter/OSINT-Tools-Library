### What does ECO-SOLVE Global Monitoring System do?

The ECO-SOLVE Global Monitoring System uses AI and OSINT methodologies to monitor online environmental crime and transform collected data into actionable intelligence. Its primary focus is illicit wildlife trade, including online advertisements and activity involving protected and threatened species.

The system forms part of the wider ECO-SOLVE programme, which aims to monitor illicit online environmental trade and produce information that can support law enforcement, policymakers and other relevant stakeholders.

Data from online wildlife-trafficking monitoring is presented through an interactive interface.

**The lowdown:** It’s a specialist environmental-crime intelligence resource rather than a general-purpose search engine.&#x20;

### Use in Reporting

ECO-SOLVE Global Monitoring System fits best during the discovery, enrichment, analysis and verification stages of the OSINT workflow. Investigators can use it after identifying a suspected wildlife-trafficking activity, species, advertisement, platform or geographic area to establish the wider environmental-crime and illicit wildlife-trade context.

It's particularly valuable for specialist wildlife-trafficking investigations, where it can provide structured data and analytical context that would be difficult to obtain through ordinary web searches. However, as always, it's not a substitute for direct source verification or broader OSINT collection.

### Ethical Considerations

* Take care when handling information concerning protected or endangered species, as publication could potentially facilitate targeting or exploitation.&#x20;
* Treat GMS records as indicators, not proof of criminality, trafficking or identity.
* Verify significant findings against the underlying source and independent evidence.
* Avoid unnecessarily collecting or recording personal information.
* Do not use GMS results alone to make decisions that could materially affect an individual.
