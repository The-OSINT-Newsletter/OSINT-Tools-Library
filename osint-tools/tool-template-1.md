### What does PSNProfiles do?

PSN Profiles is a website that collects and displays publicly available PlayStation Network trophy information, allowing users to search PlayStation usernames and view gaming activity, including trophies earned, games played, completion percentages, trophy timelines and player rankings.&#x20;

While it doesn’t reveal private PlayStation account information, it can expose publicly available activity linked to a username. For example, trophy timelines, game preferences and account activity can help build a picture of an online persona or corroborate whether two accounts may belong to the same individual.

**The lowdown:** The main value for investigators is not the gaming data itself but the potential connection between a PlayStation username and other online identities.

### Use in Reporting

PSNProfiles can be used to:

* Identify and analyse publicly available gaming usernames.
* Support online identity investigations through username correlation.
* Verify whether a username appears consistently across gaming platforms.
* Provide contextual information about an online persona.
* Establish timelines of public gaming activity.
* Support investigations involving online communities, harassment, fraud or digital identity.

Whilst not widely cited as a primary source in major investigations, publicly available gaming data and PlayStation Network information have been used in law enforcement investigations and reporting to support digital identity attribution and online activity analysis.&#x20;

### Summary

PSNProfiles is most useful during the online identity discovery and attribution stages of the OSINT workflow, helping provide additional context around an online persona, particularly when a username appears across multiple platforms. However, it should be treated as a supporting source only, with identity conclusions requiring corroboration from independent evidence such as social media profiles, registration data, activity patterns or other open-source records.

### Ownership

Owned and operated by its founder and administrator known online as [Sly Ripper](https://forum.psnprofiles.com/profile/1-sly-ripper/), under the corporate entity Gaming Profiles Ltd.

### Ethical Considerations

* Only analyse publicly available information.
* Avoid attempting to access private PlayStation accounts or restricted data.
* Do not assume a username identifies a specific individual without additional verification.
* Consider that gaming profiles may belong to minors or vulnerable individuals.
* Avoid publishing unnecessary personal details linked to gaming accounts.
