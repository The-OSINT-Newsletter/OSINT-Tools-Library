### What does AnalyzeInsta do?

AnalyzeInsta is a web-based social media intelligence and profile analysis platform designed to audit public Instagram accounts without requiring Instagram credentials. By relying strictly on public data, it allows researchers, analysts, and investigators to evaluate engagement metrics, examine posting cadence, inspect recent media, and assess account authenticity without leaving a digital footprint on Instagram.

It is particularly valuable during digital investigations and background verification where analysts must avoid alerting a target profile through active interactions or story views.

**The lowdown**: A fast, privacy-safe analytics tool for verifying and benchmarking public Instagram accounts without account contamination or password requirements.

### How to Use:

1. Navigate to the [AnalyzeInsta](https://analyzeinsta.com) website.
2. Enter the target Instagram username into the search bar (no @ symbol or password required).
3. Review the generated profile audit, including calculated engagement rate, average likes and comments, posting frequency, and recent post performance.
