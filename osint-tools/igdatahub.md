### What does IGDataHub do?

IGDataHub records the follower, following and post counts about once a day (for public Instagram accounts).&#x20;

Each account's report page (e.g. [https://igdatahub.com/blog/kyliejenner-instagram-profile-stats/](https://igdatahub.com/blog/kyliejenner-instagram-profile-stats/)) shows a 'Follower Growth Over Time' chart of those readings (hover a point for its date and count), plus engagement rate, top posts and hashtags.

/compare/ puts tracked accounts side by side, and /rankings/ lists the week's fastest-growing accounts. Other public profiles can be looked up from the homepage for followers, following and the latest post.

**The lowdown:** It’s most useful when an investigation needs more than a snapshot of an Instagram profile.

### Cookies:&#x20;

The site uses a csrftoken cookie for request-forgery protection and a sessionid cookie for application session management. Google-related cookies (FCCDCF and FCNEC) support consent-management and advertising-related functions. Tawk.to cookies (TawkConnectionTime, twk\_idm\_key and twk\_uuid\_\*) support live-chat connection management and returning-visitor recognition.

### Use in Reporting

IGDataHub can support:

* Researching public Instagram profiles and recording profile details, follower counts and verification status.
* Examining public posts, captions, hashtags, comments and available video-view counts.
* Tracking historical follower-count changes and identifying periods of notable growth or decline.
* Comparing engagement metrics across public accounts, while accounting for differences in audience size and content.
* Investigating hashtags and identifying relevant public posts or creators for follow-up research.
* Examining posting cadence and the timing of public content.
* Supporting reporting on influencer marketing, public campaigns and creator-audience relationships.
* Identifying changes that warrant further investigation, such as abrupt follower-count movements.
* Exporting available data to CSV for reproducible analysis and visualisation.

| **Capabilities**                                                                               | **Limitations**                                                                                                             |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Dated follower readings (about once a day) for tracked accounts, charted on free report pages. | History exists only for accounts IGDataHub tracks, and only from when tracking started (e.g. 24 May 2026 for @kyliejenner). |
| Engagement rate, top posts and hashtags on report pages.                                       | About one reading a day, so a change is dated to a day, not an hour.                                                        |
| Side-by-side comparison of tracked accounts.                                                   | Counts are Instagram's public totals: they cannot tell real from fake followers or say why a count moved.                   |
| Weekly fastest-growing account rankings.                                                       | Some tracked accounts have no report page yet.                                                                              |
| No Instagram login needed.                                                                     | The homepage lookup for other profiles shows counts and the latest post only, and asks for an email.                        |

### Summary

IGDataHub is a partially free Instagram OSINT tool for researching public profiles, hashtags, engagement and audience trends. Its most useful investigative feature is historical follower tracking, which can provide evidence of changes that would not be apparent from a current profile snapshot alone. It’s also useful in the timeline stage of an investigation, to date follower jumps or drops on large public Instagram accounts without logging in to Instagram.

It’s best used for discovery, collection and preliminary analysis. However, you should verify significant findings against original Instagram content and other sources, and clearly distinguish observed metrics from interpretations about an account's behaviour or intent.

### Ownership

IGDataHub ([igdatahub.com](http://igdatahub.com). No further details disclosed.

### Ethical Considerations

* Report pages concern real people. A count change says nothing about motive, so avoid implying one.
* A change that coincides with an event is not proof the event caused it.
* Use it for public, mostly large accounts; don't use follower data to target private individuals.
