### What does Flock Locations do?

Flock Locations is a public map of Flock Safety and ALPR camera locations. Each record is labeled by how it was collected, and the methodology page explains the labels: https://flocklocations.com/methodology

### How to Use:

**1. Open the map and search for a place, or browse the States and Cities directories.**

**2. Open a camera record to see its details and how it was documented.**

**3. Read the methodology page before relying on a record in reporting.**
