### What does BeenVerified do?

BeenVerified pulls together data from public records, commercial databases, and online sources to create searchable profiles on people and businesses. It's designed to help users quickly find contact details, address histories, relatives, social media accounts, and other associated records from a single search.&#x20;

**The lowdown:** It simplifies the process of locating publicly available information about individuals.

### Use in Reporting

Been Verified is useful for:

* Subject identification.
* Contact discovery.
* Historical address research.
* Relationship mapping.
* Preliminary background investigations.
* Lead generation during OSINT investigations.

| **Capabilities**                                                                                       | **Limitations**                                                               |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Conducts people searches using names, numbers, emails and physical addresses.                          | Primarily focused on U.S. records, with limited coverage outside the U.S.     |
| Identifies possible relatives, associates and household connections.                                   | Information may be outdated, incomplete, or contain inaccuracies.             |
| Provides historical address & contact information where available.                                     | Results can generate false positives, especially for those with common names. |
| Performs reverse phone and reverse email lookups.                                                      | Does not provide access to private, confidential, or protected records.       |
| Aggregates info from multiple public records/commercial data sources into a single searchable profile. | <p><br></p>                                                                   |

### Summary

BeenVerified is a popular people-search platform, particularly useful for identity resolution, contact discovery, and preliminary OSINT investigations. While it can uncover valuable leads quickly, investigators should treat results as starting points and verify important findings through authoritative sources.

### Ownership

Been Verified is a privately-held company founded by [Josh Levy ](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jmlevy/)and [Ross Cohen](https://www.linkedin.com/in/rosscoh/) in 2007 and headquartered in New York.

### Ethical Considerations

* Respect privacy and data protection laws when researching individuals.
* Avoid using information for harassment, stalking, discrimination, or unlawful surveillance.
* Understand that aggregated records may contain errors or outdated information.
* Verify findings before publication, reporting, or decision-making.
* Consider the potential impact of publishing personal information, even when publicly available.
* Use the tool only for legitimate investigative, research, or due diligence purposes.
