### What does ThisNumber do?

ThisNumber provides international phone number lookup services covering more than 200 countries. Results can include the country of origin, geographic information, line type, carrier and number validity. For US numbers, results may also include owner names, addresses and scam reports.

**The lowdown:** It can help establish basic information about an unknown phone number. <br>

### Use in Reporting

ThisNumber can be used to:

* Identify the country and region associated with a phone number.
* Identify the reported carrier and line type.
* Support investigations into unknown or suspicious callers.
* Investigate potential scam and spam numbers.
* Corroborate phone number information from other OSINT sources.
* Provide supporting information when profiling a person, organisation or digital identifier.
* Record available phone number information as a lead for further investigation.

| **Capabilities**                                      | **Limitations**                                            |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Searches phone numbers across 200+ countries.         | Results vary depending on the country.                     |

| Provides carrier and line-type information.           | International numbers may have limited results.            |

### Summary

ThisNumber is best used during the initial discovery and enrichment stages of the OSINT workflow and can provide useful context around an unknown number, including its country, geographic information, carrier, line type and potential reputation information.&#x20;

**Note:** Results should be treated as supporting intelligence rather than definitive attribution, particularly because the amount and accuracy of available information varies between countries.

### Ethical Considerations

* Use phone lookup information for legitimate investigative purposes.
* Treat results as leads rather than confirmed identity information.
* Corroborate names, addresses and ownership information with other sources.
* Avoid unnecessary exposure of personal information in reports.
* Do not use the service for prohibited screening purposes.
* Consider applicable privacy and data-protection requirements.
