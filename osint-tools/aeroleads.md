### What does Aeroleads do?

Aeroleads helps you find the people behind organisations by gathering publicly available professional information into a searchable format, making it easier to identify key personnel, business contacts, and company relationships.

While primarily designed for sales prospecting, it can also support legitimate OSINT investigations where contact identification is relevant.

**The lowdown:** It’s a great tool for quickly identifying individuals associated with organisations.

### Use in Reporting

Aeroleads can support investigations involving:

* Corporate due diligence.
* Company profiling.
* Business relationship mapping.
* Professional network analysis.
* Lead and contact identification.
* Background research on organisations and employees.

From an OSINT perspective, Aeroleads is best viewed as an enrichment tool rather than an evidential source. Any information obtained should be independently verified before inclusion in intelligence products or investigative reports.&#x20;

| **Capabilities**                                                 | **Limitations**                                                                  |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Identifies professional email addresses linked to organisations. | Data accuracy depends on publicly available information and third-party sources. |
| Provides business contact discovery at scale.                    | Contact details may be outdated/incomplete.                                      |
| Supports company and employee profiling.                         | Coverage varies by region, industry, and organisation.                           |
| Integrates with CRM and lead-management platforms.               | Free-tier functionality is limited.                                              |
| Can assist with organisational network mapping.                  | Use of personal information may be subject to privacy and data protection laws.  |
| Enables export of discovered contacts for further analysis.      | Not all results can be independently validated through the platform alone.       |

### Summary

Aeroleads is best suited to the discovery, collection, and enrichment stages of the OSINT workflow, helping identify key individuals, uncover organisational connections, and add contact-related context to existing intelligence holdings.

### Ownership

AeroLeads is owned and founded by [Pushkar Gaikwad](https://x.com/pushkargaikwad), who launched the B2B web prospecting and lead generation software in 2015 as a [“bootstrapped startup.”](https://woodpecker.co/blog/aeroleads/)

The company operates primarily out of Bengaluru, India, and San Francisco, California.&#x20;

### Ethical Considerations

* Ensure all collection activities comply with applicable privacy and data protection legislation.
* Verify contact information through independent sources before operational use.
* Avoid collecting or storing unnecessary personal information.
* Consider proportionality when researching private individuals.
* Respect organisational policies governing contact discovery and data handling.
* Be transparent about source confidence and verification status within reporting.
