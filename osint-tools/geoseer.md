### What does Geoseer do?

GeoSeer uses artificial intelligence and computer vision to analyse visual material and estimate where it was captured. It examines features such as buildings, roads, architecture, vegetation, landscapes, signage, street furniture and other environmental clues.

It provides possible location matches, including geographic coordinates, addresses, confidence information and explanations of the reasoning behind its predictions.

**The lowdown:** The platform uses a multi-agent AI workflow to investigate images and videos to estimate a location.&#x20;

### Cookies:&#x20;

GeoSeer uses first-party security and session cookies, including geoseer\_csrf, which supports Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) protection, and geoseer\_session, which maintains application session state. Both are session-scoped and use SameSite=Lax. The session cookie is additionally Secure and HttpOnly. A Cloudflare \_\_cf\_bm cookie was also observed on the third-party .paddle.com domain, supporting Paddle's bot-management and security functions during payment-related browsing.&#x20;

### Use in Reporting

GeoSeer is useful for:

* Investigating the likely location where a photograph or video was captured.
* Checking whether user-generated content corresponds to the location claimed by a news report or social media post.
* Generating geographic leads from architectural features, road layouts, vegetation, signage and landscape characteristics.
* Supporting the geolocation of conflict footage, demonstrations, accidents and other reported incidents.
* Comparing several photographs from the same event or location to identify shared geographic features.
* Investigating historical photographs and adding geographic context to previously unidentified images.
* Checking property, building and site photographs against their advertised or reported locations.
* Supporting investigations involving public imagery, online reporting and visual evidence.
* Identifying possible locations for further examination using satellite imagery, street-level imagery and mapping services.

You can [check out Geoseer’s use cases here. ](https://geoseeer.com/use-cases)

### Summary

GeoSeer is an AI-powered image and video geolocation tool with the ability to analyse multiple images and videos, making it a useful starting point when investigating visual evidence.

However, make sure to treat it as a lead-generation and investigative support tool rather than a definitive verification service. Its suggested locations need to be checked against original imagery, independent mapping sources and other corroborating evidence.

### Ownership

Developed and operated by [Shenzhen Ques Technology Co., Ltd.,](https://quesx.com/) a technology company based in Shenzhen, China.

### Ethical Considerations

* Treat AI-generated location results as investigative leads, not confirmed facts.
* Verify suggested locations using independent satellite imagery, street-level imagery, maps and other reliable sources.
* Consider whether publishing precise coordinates could expose vulnerable individuals, private residences or sensitive locations.
* Remember that an accurate-looking AI explanation can still be wrong; independently check the visual clues it relies upon.
* Be aware that publicly sharing a result can make the associated media and information accessible to others and potentially indexable by search engines.
