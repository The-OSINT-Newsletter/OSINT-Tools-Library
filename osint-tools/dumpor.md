### What does Dumpor do?

Dumpor is an Instagram viewer that provides anonymous access to publicly available Instagram content. You can view profiles, posts, reels, stories (where available), hashtags and locations without signing into Instagram.

For OSINT practitioners, it can be useful for quickly reviewing public Instagram activity, preserving contextual information, identifying linked accounts, and gathering open-source evidence while reducing the likelihood of influencing platform analytics or notifying the account owner.

**The lowdown:** It’s a simple web tool that enables quick access to public Instagram content without requiring an account.&#x20;

**Note:** As Dumpor relies on publicly accessible Instagram data, functionality can change when Instagram updates its platform or restricts third-party access.

### Cookies:&#x20;

The site uses cookies provided primarily by Yandex Metrica for website analytics, visitor statistics, session management, and GDPR compliance. Cookies such as yandexuid, yuidss, ymex, and yabs-sid are used to distinguish anonymous visitors and analyse website usage. Additional cookies (is\_gdpr, is\_gdpr\_b) support GDPR compliance, while receive-cookie-deprecation is used for browser compatibility with evolving cookie standards.&#x20;

**Note:** Be mindful of third-party ads and pop-ups on the site.

### Use in Reporting

Dumpor can be particularly useful for:

<br>

* Person of Interest (POI) profiling
* Social media investigations (SOCMINT)
* Username attribution
* Timeline reconstruction
* Digital footprint analysis
* Event and incident investigations
* Background research and due diligence
* Image and content preservation

| **Capabilities**                                                          | **Limitations**                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| View public Instagram profiles anonymously.                               | Only accesses publicly available Instagram content. Private accounts cannot be viewed.                                                |
| Browse posts, reels, hashtags, and locations without logging in.          | Functionality (e.g. story viewing) depends on Instagram’s platform and may become unavailable without notice.                         |
| Reduce the likelihood of generating profile or story view notifications.  | Content may not always be current due to indexing or caching delays.                                                                  |
| Quickly gather publicly available profile information for investigations. | Not necessarily suitable for real-time monitoring, as third-party services may experience delays or temporary loss of functionality.  |

### Summary

Dumpor is best suited to the collection stage of the OSINT workflow, with additional value during analysis. It enables investigators to anonymously identify, review and collect publicly available Instagram content before moving into deeper analysis and reporting.

### Ethical Considerations

* Only collect information that is publicly available.
* Respect privacy and avoid attempts to circumvent access restrictions on private accounts.
* Verify important findings using the original Instagram source where possible.
* Preserve evidence responsibly, including timestamps and URLs.
* Ensure collection complies with organisational policy, platform terms of service, and applicable legislation.
