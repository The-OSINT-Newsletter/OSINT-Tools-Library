### What does TryFaceSearch do?

TryFaceSearch is an AI reverse face-search tool designed to help you find where a photograph or visually similar face may appear on public webpages.

Rather than searching for a username or text phrase, you can submit an image containing an adult face. The service detects the face and, after the user consents, sends the photo to connected search providers to retrieve possible public-image matches.

**The lowdown:** It’s an image-discovery and public-source investigation tool.

### Use in Reporting

TryFaceSearch is potentially useful for:

* Investigating where a photograph appears across public webpages and indexed image sources.
* Checking whether a profile photograph may have been reused on multiple public accounts.
* Researching possible impersonation or fraudulent-profile cases by tracing the photograph's public appearances.
* Identifying public webpages containing visually similar photographs.
* Locating possible earlier appearances of an image for further provenance research.
* Comparing photographs appearing on different public profiles or webpages.
* Finding source pages that may provide captions, publication dates, usernames or other contextual information.
* Investigating whether a photograph appears in public creator pages, press coverage or other indexed material.
* Supporting image-reuse investigations by locating possible copies and associated webpages.
* Generating leads for further research into the origin and context of a photograph.

### Summary

TryFaceSearch is a reverse face-search and public-image investigation platform that helps users locate possible appearances of a photograph across public webpages.

Its main value is generating public-source leads and providing possible image matches with source links. However, a face-search result is not definitive proof of identity. Results should be checked against original webpages, publication dates, captions and independent evidence.

### Ethical Considerations

* Use only photographs you own or have permission or another lawful basis to process.
* Do not upload photographs of minors. The service explicitly prohibits searching for minors.
* Do not use face-search results for employment, credit, insurance, housing or other consequential eligibility decisions.
* A visual match does not prove identity, account ownership, relationships, location, guilt or intent.
* Similar-looking people and reused photographs can produce false positives.
* Consider the privacy implications of sending a photograph to connected search providers, including possible international processing.
* Do not assume deleting a report removes the photograph from third-party providers or original webpages.
* Opt-out exists for removal requests.
