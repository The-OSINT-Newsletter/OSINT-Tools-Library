### What does Australia Public Record do?

Australia Public Record is a useful starting point for Australian OSINT investigations as it aggregates publicly available information and points investigators towards relevant records, databases, and sources that may otherwise take significant time to locate manually. Record types include public records, people finder, birth and death records, marriage and divorce records, and immigration records,

**The lowdown:** It acts as a gateway to Australian public records, helping users locate relevant information and external record sources through a centralised search interface.

### Summary

Australia Public Record simplifies the process of locating publicly available records and identifying potential leads, but investigators should treat it as a discovery tool rather than a definitive source. Always verify results through primary records.
