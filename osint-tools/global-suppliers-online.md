### What does Global Suppliers Online do?

Global Suppliers Online is an international B2B directory that lists businesses involved in manufacturing, wholesale, export and international trade. Companies create profiles advertising their products and services, allowing buyers to search for suppliers by product category, country or business type.&#x20;

The platform can provide useful information about a company's declared business activities, contact details, product ranges, export markets and manufacturing capabilities. It can also help identify commercial relationships and potential supply chain links.&#x20;

**The lowdown:** Whilst not an authoritative company register, it can provide valuable leads during supply chain, procurement and corporate investigations.

### Use in Reporting

Global Suppliers online is useful for:

* Identifying manufacturers and suppliers.
* Supporting supply chain investigations.
* Profiling businesses and commercial activity.
* Discovering product and export information.
* Corroborating company identities.
* Generating investigative leads.

The platform is primarily used as a lead-generation and corroboration tool during company and supply chain investigations.&#x20;

| **Capabilities**                                             | **Limitations**                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Searches suppliers across multiple countries and industries. | Company information is largely self-submitted.                 |
| Identifies manufacturers, exporters, and wholesalers.        | Profiles may be outdated or incomplete.                        |
| Provides company contact details and product information.    | Doesn’t verify company legitimacy or legal status.             |
| Supports supply chain and procurement investigations.        | Limited information compared with official business registers. |
| No account needed for basic searching.                       | May contain duplicate or inactive company listings.            |

### Summary

Global Suppliers Online is most valuable during the discovery and collection phases of the OSINT workflow. Although it is not an authoritative company register, it can provide valuable leads during supply chain, procurement and corporate investigations.

### Ownership

### Ethical Considerations

* Verify self-declared company information using authoritative sources.
* Avoid assuming a listed supplier relationship reflects an active commercial partnership.
* Respect applicable data protection and commercial confidentiality requirements.
* Clearly distinguish between directory listings and verified corporate records.
* Consider the age of listings when assessing relevance.
