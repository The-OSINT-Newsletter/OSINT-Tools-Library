### What does MW Geofind do?

MW Geofind pulls in geotagged YouTube videos and plots them on an interactive map. This makes it much easier to find relevant footage from a specific location, especially useful during breaking news, conflict monitoring, or event verification. Instead of searching blindly on YouTube, you can visually explore what was filmed in a given area.&#x20;

**The lowdown:** It’s a fast, simple tool for finding location-based YouTube footage.

### Use in Reporting

MW Geofind is useful for:

* Finding eyewitness footage near an incident.
* Supporting geolocation efforts.
* Building timelines from user-generated content.
* Corroborating events with visual evidence.

In real-world terms, it’s an ideal tool for reporting on conflict coverage (e.g. locating YouTube footage from areas in Ukraine or Iran), protest and civil unrest monitoring (finding on-the-ground uploads near events) and breaking news incidents.&#x20;

| **Capabilities**                         | **Limitations**                                    |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Maps geotagged YouTube content visually. | Only works with videos that have geolocation data. |
| Filters by date, keyword, and radius.    | Dependent on YouTube’s API and available metadata. |
| Surfaces hyper-local video evidence.     | Can miss relevant videos without location tags.    |
| Speeds up discovery of relevant footage. | <p><br></p>                                        |

### Summary

MW Geofind is best used during the Collection and Initial Analysis phases of the OSINT workflow as a discovery layer alongside other verification tools.
