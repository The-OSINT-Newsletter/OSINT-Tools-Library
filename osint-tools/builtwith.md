### What does BuiltWith do?

BuiltWith analyses publicly observable website code and technical indicators to identify technologies associated with a domain. Its database covers a broad range of technologies, including:

* Content management systems (CMS).
* E-commerce platforms and payment-related technologies.
* JavaScript frameworks and web development libraries.
* Web analytics and audience-measurement tools.
* Advertising, marketing and tracking technologies.
* Content delivery networks (CDNs) and web-server technologies.
* Hosting-related and other digital infrastructure indicators.
* Historical technology usage and adoption trends.

Paid services can provide more extensive domain reports, technology-based lists, historical information, exports and API access.

**The lowdown:** It can help establish what technologies a website appears to use, identify possible links between websites through shared technical indicators, and generate leads about a website's development, commercial services or changes over time.

**Important note:** A shared technology does not establish common ownership or prove that two websites are operated by the same person. Findings should be treated as indicators for further investigation.

### Use in Reporting

BuiltWith can help:

* Identify technologies associated with a website.
* Document the apparent technical footprint of a domain.
* Compare the technology stacks of websites associated with an organisation or investigation.
* Generate leads about shared analytics, marketing, e-commerce or other technology providers.
* Examine changes in a website's detected technologies over time, where historical records are available.
* Investigate suspicious websites, possible impersonation sites and questionable online services.
* Support technical reconnaissance and preliminary assessments of a website's digital infrastructure.
* Provide context for research into online businesses, technology adoption and website development.
* Identify technical indicators that can be checked against DNS records, certificates, archived pages and other sources.
* Support market and competitive research into the technologies used by organisations.

As a real-world example, [researchers at The Influence Industry Project ](https://influenceindustry.org/en/explorer/case-studies/investigating-compromised-websites/)investigated why the defunct Cambridge Analytica website had reappeared with suspicious adverts and links. They used BuiltWith to identify other websites apparently redirecting to it, helping investigate possible website spoofing and commercial exploitation.

### Summary

BuiltWith is a website technology profiling and internet research tool that identifies technologies associated with publicly accessible websites. Its strongest analytical value is in the analysis and processing stage of the OSINT workflow, where the collected technology data is compared and interpreted. &#x20;

The platform can identify potential links between websites, but those links need corroboration before being reported as evidence of common ownership or coordination. Keep in mind that technology detections may also be incomplete or outdated.

### Ownership

BuiltWith Pty Ltd is an Australian company headquartered in Sydney and founded by Gary Brewer.

### Ethical Considerations

* Treat detected technologies as indicators rather than definitive proof of a website's current configuration or operator.
* Distinguish shared technical infrastructure from evidence of common ownership, coordination or control.
* Consider the possibility of false positives, stale detections and changes that have not yet been reflected in BuiltWith's database.
* Do not infer malicious intent solely from a website's technology stack or its use of a particular hosting, analytics or e-commerce provider.
* Avoid publishing unsupported claims about individuals or organisations based on technology overlap alone.
* Respect privacy and applicable data-protection laws when processing information about identifiable people or business contacts.
