### What does AnyWho do?

AnyWho allows you to search for people, businesses, phone numbers, and addresses (in the US) using information from public directory sources. It can assist with basic identity research, contact discovery, and linking publicly available identifiers such as names, locations, and phone numbers.

**The lowdown:** It’s a simple people-search tool that works best as an early-stage discovery resource.

### Cookies:&#x20;

AnyWho uses a combination of functional, analytics, and third-party advertising cookies. First-party cookies (Anywho-IDKEY, Anywho-SESSIONKEY) support visitor identification, session management, and website functionality. Google Analytics cookies (\_ga\_4YLQR6FQ93) collect website usage statistics. Third-party DoubleClick cookies (IDE, DSID, ar\_debug) indicate the use of Google's advertising and measurement services, which may support personalised advertising and visitor tracking.

### Use in Reporting

AnyWho can support reporting by:

* Providing initial leads on individuals and contact details.
* Helping identify possible links between names, addresses, and phone numbers.
* Supporting identity resolution when combined with other sources.
* Providing publicly available information that can assist with investigative timelines.

Use cases may include people-finding investigations, fraud and scam investigations, cyber investigations, and missing persons cases.

| **Capabilities**                                          | **Limitations**                                                               |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Searches publicly available people directory information. | Data accuracy depends on directory records and update frequency.              |
| Supports name, phone number, and address searches.        | Coverage is limited outside the United States.                                |
| Useful for initial identity discovery.                    | Results may contain outdated or incorrect information.                        |
| Can help  connect basic personal identifiers.             | Doesn’t provide verified identity confirmation.                               |
| Provides quick results suitable for early OSINT research. | Limited investigative depth compared with specialist people-search platforms. |

### Summary

AnyWho is a basic people-search and directory lookup tool that fits best within the initial discovery and identity research stages of the OSINT workflow.
