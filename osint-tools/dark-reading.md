### What does Dark Reading do?

Dark Reading is one of the most widely recognised cybersecurity news platforms, covering topics such as ransomware, data breaches, nation-state activity, vulnerabilities, malware, cloud security, critical infrastructure, and cybercrime. It aggregates reporting from security researchers, vendors, government agencies, and industry experts.

**The lowdown:** It’s a valuable source for understanding emerging threats and the wider cybersecurity landscape.

### Use in Reporting

Dark Reading is ideal for:&#x20;

* Providing context for cyber incidents, vulnerabilities, and emerging threats.
* Supporting background sections and situational awareness in intelligence reports.
* Identifying timelines, trends, and significant developments.
* Referencing expert analysis and industry commentary where appropriate.
* Helping identify additional primary sources for verification.

| **Capabilities**                                                | **Limitations**                                                                     |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Reports on current cybersecurity news and incidents.            | Primarily a news and analysis platform rather than an investigative tool.           |
| Covers vulnerabilities, malware, ransomware, and threat actors. | Some articles reference third-party research that should be independently verified. |
| Provides expert analysis and industry commentary.               | Coverage depends on publicly reported information.                                  |
| Includes interviews, research, and technical insights.          | Not all technical details or indicators of compromise are included.                 |
| Offers searchable archives of cybersecurity reporting.          | Some content may require registration.                                              |

### Summary

Dark Reading is best used during the collection phase of the OSINT workflow to gather current cybersecurity news and threat intelligence, and during analysis to provide context, trends, and background to investigative findings. While it provides great context and analysis, it’s not a primary source of technical evidence so findings should be independently verified.

### Ownership

Owned by Informa TechTarget, a global technology media and information services company. The CFO and Treasurer is listed as Daniel Noreck.

### Ethical Considerations

* Distinguish news reporting from verified evidence.
* Corroborate significant claims using official or primary sources.
* Ensure cited information is current, as cyber threats evolve rapidly.
* Respect copyright when quoting or reproducing articles.
* Avoid relying on a single media source for intelligence assessments.
