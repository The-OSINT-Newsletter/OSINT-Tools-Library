### What does BoardReader do?

BoardReader is a specialised search engine designed to index content from online forums, message boards and discussion communities. Unlike traditional search engines, it focuses on user-generated discussions, making it particularly useful for locating niche conversations, technical advice, historical posts and community commentary.

You can search for keywords, usernames, organisations, or topics to identify discussions across thousands of forums.

**The lowdown:** The platform can uncover information that may not rank highly in conventional search results, providing valuable context and additional investigative leads.

### Use in Reporting

BoardReader is useful for:&#x20;

* Identifying historical discussions relating to an individual or organisation.
* Supporting attribution through forum usernames and aliases.
* Assessing public sentiment or community reactions.
* Locating technical discussions relevant to cyber investigations.
* Discovering additional identifiers for investigative pivoting.
* Corroborating information using multiple independent OSINT sources.

Example operational use cases:

* Threat intelligence reports identifying forum discussions linked to cyber activity.
* Brand or reputation monitoring using community discussions.
* Attribution reports linking usernames across multiple forums.
* Background research supporting investigations into fraud or extremist narratives.

| **Capabilities**                                                         | **Limitations**                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Searches thousands of online forums and message boards.                  | Coverage depends on which forums are indexed.                       |
| Finds historical discussions often missed by traditional search engines. | Some forums restrict indexing or require login access.              |
| Supports keyword, phrase, and username searches.                         | Search results may contain outdated or deleted discussions.         |
| Useful for discovering aliases and online identities.                    | User-generated content may be inaccurate, biased, or misleading.    |
| Provides additional context through community discussions.               | Limited advanced filtering compared with mainstream search engines. |

### Summary

BoardReader is best used during the collection and enrichment stages of the OSINT workflow, when you’re gathering publicly available information and identifying additional leads for corroboration.&#x20;

**Note:** As forum content is user-generated, findings should always be validated against independent sources before being relied upon in intelligence reporting.

### Ethical Considerations

* Respect forum terms of service and community rules.
* Consider the context and date of historical discussions.
* Avoid publishing personal information unless operationally justified.
* Corroborate findings using multiple independent sources.
* Be mindful that forum posts may represent opinion rather than verified fact.
