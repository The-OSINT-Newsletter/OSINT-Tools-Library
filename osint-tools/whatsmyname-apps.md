### What does WhatsMyName Apps do?

WhatsMyName Apps accepts a username and checks public websites for potential matching accounts. Results can include blocked or unknown checks.&#x20;

**Note:** A matching username is not evidence that accounts belong to the same person. The website and Android app are independent of the original WhatsMyName project.

**The lowdown:** Its primary purpose is username discovery and digital-footprint research.

### Use in Reporting

WhatsMyName Apps can be useful for:

* Identifying potential public profiles associated with a username across social networks, forums, gaming platforms and other websites.
* Discovering possible cross-platform accounts that may warrant further investigation.
* Comparing username variants, including alternative spellings, underscores, dots and numbers.
* Locating public profile pages and links that can be examined directly for additional context.
* Identifying potential links between accounts through independently corroborated profile photographs, biographies, linked websites or posting history.
* Exploring a subject's publicly visible digital footprint by organising potential account matches across multiple platforms.
* Documenting the status of each check, including found, not found, blocked and unknown results.
* Exporting results to JSON or CSV for case notes, further analysis or inclusion in an investigative workflow.
* Recording the search date, query variants, source URLs and verification steps to support transparent and reproducible reporting.
* Identifying gaps and uncertainties in a username search, including platforms that blocked automated checks or returned ambiguous responses.

### Summary

WhatsMyName Apps is a free browser-based starting point for username discovery, most useful for generating leads early in an investigation. Its broad coverage, multiple search variants, profile previews and export options make it useful for preliminary digital-footprint research and cross-platform account discovery.

However, Identity attribution and profile availability require manual corroboration, and blocked checks and uncertain results limit conclusions.

### Ethical Considerations

* Search only for a lawful, proportionate purpose.
* Consider privacy before submitting a username to an external server.
* Do not infer identity from a username match alone.
* Avoid harassment or exposing personal information.
* Clearly separate the tool's automated results from independently verified observations and analyst conclusions.
* The service states that it is not a consumer reporting agency and must not be used for employment, housing, credit, insurance or other decisions covered by the US Fair Credit Reporting Act or similar laws.
* Corroborate evidence before publication.
