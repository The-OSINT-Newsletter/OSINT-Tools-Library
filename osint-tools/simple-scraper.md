### What does Simple Scraper do?

Simple Scraper automates the collection of information from websites and converts it into usable datasets. Instead of manually copying information line by line, users can extract hundreds or thousands of records in minutes.

It’s simple to use; available as a browser extension and cloud-based service, enabling users to collect structured data from web pages without needing programming skills.&#x20;

**The lowdown:** It bridges the gap between manual collection and advanced coding-based scraping tools.

### Summary

Simple Scraper is primarily a collection tool that helps investigators efficiently gather and structure large amounts of publicly available web data for later analysis and reporting. While it’s not a replacement for advanced scraping tools in every scenario, it provides a great balance of power, speed, and ease of use.

### Ethical Considerations

* Avoid excessive requests that may impact website performance.
* Ensure compliance with applicable privacy and data protection laws.
* Only collect information necessary for the investigation or research objective.
* Consider whether individuals would reasonably expect the information to be collected and analysed.
* Avoid collecting sensitive personal data without lawful justification.
* Use automation responsibly and proportionately.
