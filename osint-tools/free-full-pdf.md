### What does Free Full PDF do?

FreeFullPDF is a search engine designed to improve access to open-access scientific journals, theses, posters and patents. It covers a broad range of scientific subjects to help users find material available in PDF format.

The service has also been described as providing full-text searching across PDF material, allowing searches to identify relevant terms within academic documents rather than relying only on titles and metadata.

**The lowdown:** It’s essentially a specialist academic discovery tool rather than a general web search engine.&#x20;

### Use in Reporting

FreeFullPDF can be useful for:

* Locating academic papers, theses, patents and other scientific material relevant to an investigation.
* Providing supporting evidence and expert research for reports, briefings and analytical assessments.
* Researching technical subjects, scientific claims, organisations, individuals and emerging areas of research.
* Identifying publicly available copies of research that may be difficult to locate through general search engines.
* Corroborating claims by locating independent academic or technical sources.&#x20;

The platform is also often used in published research and literature reviews to locate scientific articles and other full-text material, including studies not found through other academic databases.&#x20;

### Summary

FreeFullPDF is a specialist academic OSINT search tool that fits best in the discovery and collection stages of the OSINT workflow, where it can be used to identify relevant research, theses, patents, posters and full-text papers.&#x20;

It’s particularly valuable when an investigation involves a scientific, technical, medical, environmental, engineering or academic subject, but should not be treated as a comprehensive academic database.

### Ethical Considerations

* Check the copyright and licensing status of documents before reproducing or redistributing them.
* Do not assume that a PDF's availability makes every use of its contents legally unrestricted.
* Verify authorship, publication details and provenance before citing material as evidence.
* Treat academic publications as sources to be assessed rather than automatically accepted as factual.
* Avoid entering sensitive investigative queries or personal information into third-party search services.
* Where possible, cite the original publisher, journal, repository or author rather than relying solely on FreeFullPDF as the source.
