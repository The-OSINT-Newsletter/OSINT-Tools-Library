### What does Govsky do?

Govsky aggregates publicly available Bluesky accounts associated with governments and public-sector organisations. By organising them geographically, it simplifies the discovery of official voices on an emerging social media platform that is otherwise harder to systematically search.&#x20;

**The lowdown:** It’s a great free tool for easy tracking of official communications om Bluesky.

### Use in Reporting

Govsky can support:

* Identification of official government Bluesky accounts.
* Monitoring public-sector messaging and announcements.
* Tracking digital diplomacy and communications strategies.
* Verifying whether a Bluesky account is linked to a government body.

Govsky-style tracking of government Bluesky accounts reflects a broader OSINT use case where investigators monitor official social media presence to analyse state messaging and public reaction.&#x20;

For example, [researchers and journalists have tracked the rollout of U.S. government accounts on Bluesky, such as ICE and White House profiles](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/20/ice-becomes-one-of-the-most-blocked-accounts-on-bluesky-after-its-verification/), to study engagement, verification, and public backlash, including large-scale blocking trends.&#x20;

Tools like Govsky support this by making it easier to identify and monitor those accounts across countries, enabling comparative analysis of government communication on emerging platforms.

| **Capabilities**                                              | **Limitations**                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Directory of government Bluesky accounts by country.          | Limited to Bluesky (does not cover other platforms).               |

| Enables monitoring of official communications.                | May not include all government entities or newly created profiles. |
| Helps identify emerging government presence on new platforms. | Requires manual verification of account authenticity.              |

### Summary

Govsky is particularly useful for tracking official communication on emerging platforms, though it should be used alongside other tools for verification and deeper analysis.

### Ethical Considerations

* Ensure accurate attribution of accounts before reporting.
* Avoid misidentifying unofficial or parody accounts as government sources.
* Respect platform terms of service.
* Use findings responsibly, particularly in political or sensitive contexts.
