### What does the tool do?

Recon coordinates research from known subject identifiers rather than replacing the analyst's judgment. Its public interface separates People/Enterprise Search, Fraud Controls and CyberSec OSINT. Availability depends on jurisdiction, identifiers, entitlements and upstream sources.

### How to use

1. Open the public site and select the relevant workspace.
2. In people/company research, choose the country profile or International/Unknown mode and supply identifiers you are authorized to investigate.
3. In CyberSec OSINT, enter a supported domain, IP, optional port or complete HTTP(S) URL. A full URL is distinct from a host-only investigation.
4. Review provenance, observation dates, match uncertainty and unavailable checks. Corroborate important findings against the original source.

### Data processing and limitations

- This is a hosted service, not a claim that the software source code is openly licensed.
- Do not submit sensitive personal data without a lawful purpose and the appropriate authority.
- A name match is not verified identity; a threat-intelligence report is not proof of compromise.
- Missing or unavailable results are not a clean/safe verdict.
- Cyber observations are passive, point-in-time research, not a continuous SOC feed or active port scan.
- Review the site's current privacy notice, retention rules and service agreement before use.
