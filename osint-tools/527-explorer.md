### What does 527 Explorer do?

527 Explorer provides a searchable map of US 527 political organisations, which are tax-exempt groups that influence elections but are not officially tied to candidates or parties.&#x20;

The tool shows who funds such groups, how much money flows through them, and where that money is going.&#x20;

**The lowdown:** It’s essentially a financial transparency lens on political influence groups.

### Use in Reporting

527 Explorer is used to:

* Investigate political influence networks.
* Identify funding sources behind advocacy campaigns.
* Track election-related spending patterns.
* Support transparency in political finance reporting.
* Corroborate claims about donor influence.

The tool is widely used in investigative reporting as a ProPublica campaign finance data tool, but is usually cited indirectly as part of ProPublica’s political finance datasets. [See recent example here](https://www.facebook.com/reel/1546136270241138) from DW News entitled “From kings and aristocrats to Jeff Bezos or Elon Musk: How the rich avoid taxes.”

| **Capabilities**                             | **Limitations**                                                           |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Searchable database of US 527 organisations. | Only covers registered 527 organisations (not all political money flows). |
| Donor and expenditure breakdowns.            | Dependent on publicly filed financial disclosures (delayed reporting).    |
| Organisation-level financial profiling.      | Limited content on intent/informal coordination.                          |
| Historical funding patterns.                 | US-focused only.                                                          |
| Network tracing between political groups.    | Cannot capture off-books or unreported financial activity.                |

### Summary

527 Explorer is best used in the analysis and verification stages of OSINT investigations focused on political finance and influence networks, where it helps map and validate how money flows between organisations.

### Ownership

Created and maintained by [ProPublica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ProPublica), an American non-profit investigative journalism organisation based in New York.

### Ethical Considerations

* Data relates to political activity so handle carefully to avoid partisan framing.
* Be mindful of the risk of misinterpreting lawful donations as wrongdoing.
* Use for transparency, not targeting individuals.
* Consider the context of legal political advocacy.
* Avoid drawing conclusions without corroborating evidence.
