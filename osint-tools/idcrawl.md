### What does IDCrawl do?

IDCrawl aggregates publicly available information from social media platforms, search engines, public records, and other online sources to identify profiles and digital footprints associated with a person or username. For journalists and investigators, the platform can rapidly surface online accounts that might otherwise require searching multiple platforms individually.&#x20;

**The lowdown:** It’s ideal for consolidating publicly available results into a single search interface.

### Cookies:&#x20;

IDCrawl uses a mix of Google Analytics tracking, AWS bot protection, and standard web security/session cookies (XSRF + session tokens) to track usage, secure requests, and manage user sessions.

### Use in Reporting

IDCrawl is useful for:&#x20;

* Discovering social media profiles.
* Identifying username reuse across platforms.
* Finding online aliases and alternative identities.
* Building an initial digital footprint.
* Generating investigative leads.
* Supporting source verification and background research.

IDCrawl is widely used in OSINT workflows and tool guides, but it is rarely cited in formal investigations/published reports as a primary source, serving mainly as an early-stage lead generation tool rather than evidential material.&#x20;

| **Capabilities**                                                  | **Limitations**                                                   |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Searches names and usernames across multiple online sources.      | Only accesses publicly available and indexed information.         |
| Identifies social media profiles linked to a person.              | Results may contain false positives, especially for common names. |
| Helps discover reused usernames and aliases.                      | Coverage varies significantly between platforms.                  |
| Aggregates publicly available web references into one interface.  | Some advanced functionality requires payment.                     |
| Supports quick digital footprint discovery.                       | Findings require independent verification before publication.     |

### Summary

IDCrawl is particularly valuable during the early stages of investigations when researchers are attempting to establish a target's digital footprint, although the platform should be viewed as a lead-generation tool rather than a source of verified information.

### Ownership

IDCrawl is operated by [IDCrawl LLC](https://www.idcrawl.com/privacy), a US-based company that provides people-search and online identity discovery services.&#x20;

### Ethical Considerations

* Respect privacy and data-protection laws when conducting searches.
* Publicly available information should not automatically be considered accurate.
* Avoid using discovered information for harassment, doxxing, or intimidation.
* Verify identities carefully before making conclusions.
* Consider proportionality and public interest when using personal information in reporting.
* Be aware that profile aggregation tools can sometimes create misleading associations.
