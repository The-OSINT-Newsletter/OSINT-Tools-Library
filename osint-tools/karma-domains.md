### What does Karma Domains do?

Karma.Domains aggregates expired and dropping domains from 40 auction sources (13M+ domains, 400,000+ new per day) with 90+ filters.&#x20;

Free WHOIS lookup and domain expiry checkers help investigators check registration details and expiry dates. A REST API and an MCP server (31 tools) are also available.

**The lowdown:** Its most useful OSINT feature is the ability to combine a domain's archived history with backlink, traffic, and technical indicators.

### Use in Reporting

Karma.Domains can be useful for:

* Investigating a website's historical content and identifying changes in subject matter, branding or purpose.
* Identifying domains that may have been repurposed after expiry or ownership changes.
* Examining archived evidence of suspicious redirects, doorway pages, spam or other potentially harmful content.
* Comparing historical website snapshots to establish a chronology of changes.
* Identifying backlink sources and referring domains that may provide leads to related websites or organisations.
* Checking for historical links from news organisations, Wikipedia or other notable sources.
* Investigating domains that share supported analytics or advertising identifiers.
* Reviewing domain age, archive coverage, DNS/WHOIS data and other available technical indicators.

| **Capabilities**                                                                                     | **Limitations**                                                                                  |

### Summary

Karma.Domains is a domain-history and analytics platform that combines archived website content, backlink data, traffic estimates, technical checks and domain-market listings. Its most valuable OSINT applications are tracing changes in a website's purpose, investigating potentially repurposed domains, examining backlink relationships, and generating leads about related web properties.

The platform is especially useful during discovery and preliminary analysis. Findings should be checked against original Wayback Machine snapshots, current technical records, and independent sources.&#x20;

### Ethical Considerations

* Avoid unsupported accusations. A spam score, suspicious redirect or historical association does not prove criminal conduct.
* Distinguish a domain from its owner: domain history can span multiple registrants, hosting providers and unrelated organisations.
* A missing snapshot does not prove that a website did not exist or that its content was absent.
* Respect access and usage terms. Use the documented Public API/MCP or other expressly authorised access methods for automation. The terms prohibit scraping and programmatic access through private or undocumented website endpoints.
