### What does dilisense do?

dilisense consolidates information from 9,000+ official sources across sanctions, PEP, criminal watchlists and adverse media, allowing you to screen a person or organisation from a single interface. It covers sources including OFAC, the EU, UN Security Council, UK HMT and other national/regulatory sources.

It’s primarily designed for AML/KYC and compliance screening, but the searchable data can also be useful during OSINT investigations when establishing whether an individual or organisation appears on sanctions, PEP or criminal-watchlist sources.

**The lowdown:** It’s a centralised risk-screening tool that’s useful for quickly checking whether an investigative subject appears in relevant official or risk databases.

### Cookies:&#x20;

The site uses Stripe payment/security cookies, Google Analytics cookies, advertising/conversion cookies (Google/Reddit), and a cookie-consent management cookie. These primarily support payment security, website analytics, advertising attribution and management of cookie preferences.

### Use in Reporting

dilisense can be used to:

* Identify whether an individual or organisation appears on sanctions lists, PEP lists or criminal watchlists.
* Identify relevant risk indicators associated with an investigative subject.
* Cross-reference names against multiple international and national sources from a single interface.
* Identify the underlying source/list associated with a potential match.
* Support identity-resolution by comparing names and other available identifiers.
* Corroborate information obtained through other OSINT sources.
* Support AML/KYC, due-diligence and risk-assessment investigations.

The UK Government's [Economic Crime Survey 2024](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/economic-crime-survey-2024/economic-crime-survey-2024) explicitly mentions dilisense as a third-party service used by surveyed businesses for sanctions and PEP screening.

**Important Note:** a dilisense match should not automatically be treated as proof that the result relates to your subject. Similar names can produce false positives, so the result should be checked against identifiers such as date of birth, nationality, location, organisation, aliases or other available information.&#x20;

### Summary

dilisense is a useful risk-intelligence and screening tool for quickly checking people and organisations against consolidated sanctions, PEP, criminal-watchlist and adverse-media sources. It fits particularly well into the verification and analysis stages of an OSINT investigation, but significant findings should always be corroborated against the original source.

### Ownership

Owned by dilisense GmbH, based in Switzerland, with operations/teams in Zurich and Luxembourg.

### Ethical Considerations

* Avoid treating a name-only match as confirmation of identity.
* Where possible, follow a dilisense result back to the original government or regulatory source.
* A sanctions, PEP or watchlist entry does not by itself establish criminality or wrongdoing.
* Only collect and retain information necessary for the legitimate investigation.
* Preserve the source, URL, date/time and relevant identifiers supporting your finding.
* Consider false positives: Names, transliterations and aliases can produce misleading matches.
