### What does Waybien do?

Waybien is an emerging, powerful OSINT search engine for messaging and social media groups. It streamlines discovery across Telegram, Facebook, Discord, and WhatsApp.&#x20;

**The lowdown:** Whilst it cannot access private content, it saves hours of manual searching by consolidating public group information into one place.

### How to Use:

**1. Open**[ **Waybien**](https://waybien.com/en) **in your browser and enter a keyword or topic into the search bar.**

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/unknown.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**2. Filter by platform (Telegram, Facebook, Discord, or WhatsApp to narrow results).**

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/unknown (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**3. Explore results and access groups by using publicly shared join links to enter groups where available.**

Tip: Use specific keywords to avoid overly broad results, and cross-reference results for accuracy.

## Data Processing

### Account Required:

* [x] Yes
* [x] No

No for basic searches. Yes to unlock full tracking and export features.

### Cookies:&#x20;

Waybien uses cookies primarily to manage user sessions, remember preferences, and track usage for analytics and security. These include session identifiers, localisation settings, and analytics/marketing cookies from Google.

### Use in Reporting

Waybien data can be used to:

* Document public group activity across multiple messaging platforms.
* Track community trends and emerging topics.
* Identify influential groups for further OSINT investigation.
* Include metadata like group size and activity metrics in reports or dashboards.

| **Capabilities**                                                                  | **Limitations**                                                            |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Search for public groups across Telegram, Facebook, Discord, and Whatsapp.        | Cannot access private or closed group content.                             |
| Filter groups by keywords, topic, or platform.                                    | Limited historical or post-level data.                                     |
| Display metadata such as group description, activity level, and membership count. | Accuracy depends on platform-provided public information.                  |
| Provides publicly available join links where permitted.                           | Restricted queries/platform coverage on the free tier.                     |
| Track communities and trends without logging into each platform separately.       | Some platforms may block access or limit API results, reducing visibility. |

### Summary

Waybien helps analysts quickly discover active communities, trends, and conversations, most useful during the collection and analysis phases of the OSINT workflow, although it’s limited to publicly accessible groups.

### Ethical Considerations

* Only analyse publicly accessible groups.
* Do not attempt to bypass privacy settings or access closed communities.
* Respect platform terms of service and user privacy.
* Consider potential sensitivity of group content when reporting.
