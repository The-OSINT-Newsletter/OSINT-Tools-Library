### What does Vehicle AI do?

Vehicle AI uses AI to help identify and analyse vehicles. By uploading an image of any vehicle, Advanced AI will instantly recognise the make, model, year, and more.&#x20;

**The lowdown:** It’s useful for speeding up analysis but results should always be validated against trusted official or primary sources.

### Use in Reporting

Vehicle AI can be useful for:

* Vehicle identification in investigations
* Supporting OSINT reporting workflows
* Linking vehicle data across incidents
* Fraud, surveillance, or compliance checks

**Important:** Outputs should not be treated as definitive evidence. Always verify through independent sources.

| **Capabilities**                                            | **Limitations**                                      |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| AI-assisted vehicle identification.                         | Accuracy depends on underlying data sources.         |
| Data enrichment from partial inputs.                        | Limited transparency on datasets used.               |
| Potential cross-referencing of vehicle signals/data points. | Not a replacement for official vehicle registries.   |
| Faster OSINT-style vehicle investigations.                  | Potential false positives/uncertain matches.         |
| Structured investigative outputs.                           | Ownership and data provenance not clearly disclosed. |

### Summary

Vehicle AI sits best in the early-to-mid investigation stage of an OSINT workflow, where you’re turning a rough vehicle identifier into something structured and actionable.

In practice, it’s most useful at the data enrichment and pivoting stage. You’d typically feed it a number plate, partial plate, or vehicle image-derived identifier, then use its output to generate leads (locations, possible matches, associated records) that can be cross-checked elsewhere.

### Ethical Considerations

* Risk of misuse for tracking or surveillance.
* Must be used in compliance with local data protection laws.
* Outputs should not be used for harassment, stalking, or unauthorised monitoring.
