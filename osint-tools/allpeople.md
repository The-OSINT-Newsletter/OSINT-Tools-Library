### What does AllPeople do?

AllPeople is a people-search and contact aggregation platform that collects publicly available professional information about individuals and businesses in the USA. It’s designed to help users find contact details, job roles, company associations, and sometimes email or phone information linked to professionals across different industries.

**The lowdown:** It’s great for fast identity resolution and mapping relationships between people and companies.&#x20;

### Use in Reporting

AllPeople can be used to:&#x20;

* Identify individuals linked to organisations or companies.
* Support attribution research in OSINT investigations.
* Map professional relationships and organisational structures.
* Corroborate contact details found in other data sources.
* Assist in due diligence and background research.
* Provide leads for further verification using additional OSINT tools.

**Note:** The tool should be used as a supplementary lookup tool with findings requiring verification via authoritative sources.

| **Capabilities**                                                    | **Limitations**                                 |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Searches aggregated public professional profiles.                   | Data accuracy depends on third-party sources.   |
| Identifies job titles and company affiliations.                     | Information may be outdated/incomplete.         |
| Helps map organisational relationships.                             | Not all individuals are listed or discoverable. |
| Provides potential contact details (emails/phones where available). | May include duplicate or mismatched profiles.   |
| Useful for linking individuals to corporate entities.               | Limited verifications of contact details.       |

### Summary

In OSINT workflows, AllPeople can be used to map organisational structures, identify individuals linked to companies, and support attribution research by connecting names, roles, and business affiliations. However, the accuracy of data depends on the original sources it aggregates from.

### Ownership

AllPeople is operated as a private data aggregation and people-search platform under the AllPeople brand. Public corporate ownership details are limited but it’s located in the US.

### Ethical Considerations

* Only use publicly available and lawful data sources.
* Avoid using data for harassment or unsolicited contact.
* Verify identity before making attribution claims.
* Be cautious of outdated or incorrect profile data.
* Do not treat aggregated data as definitive proof of identity.
