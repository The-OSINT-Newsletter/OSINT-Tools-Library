### What does LiveATC do?

LiveATC provides live and archived audio feeds of air traffic control communications from airports and airspace around the world. Audio is collected from a global network of volunteer-operated radio receivers that capture publicly broadcast VHF aviation frequencies.

You can monitor communications between pilots and air traffic controllers, including taxi clearances, departures, arrivals, emergency responses and other operational activity. Many major airports also provide archive recordings, allowing investigators to review historical communications after an incident.

**The lowdown:** While flight tracking services show where an aircraft travelled, LiveATC can reveal why it was diverted, delayed or prioritised, as well as communications relating to emergencies, security incidents or unusual operations.

### Use in Reporting

Live ATC is useful in reporting particularly when you want to:

* Corroborate aircraft movements using real-time ATC communications.
* Investigate aviation incidents, diversions and emergency responses.
* Verify departure, arrival and taxi timings alongside flight tracking data.
* Identify operational issues such as runway closures, weather disruptions or airspace restrictions.
* Support timeline reconstruction during aviation-related investigations.

As a light, tangible example, Live ATC was [cited in the LA Times ](https://www.latimes.com/california/story/2021-07-29/possible-jetpack-man-in-sight-lax-air-traffic-control-reports)as recordings were used to report on pilot communications describing a suspected "jetpack man" near Los Angeles International Airport.&#x20;

| **Capabilities**                                                                       | **Limitations**                                                                                     |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Streams live air traffic control communications from thousands of airports worldwide.  | Coverage depends on volunteer-operated receivers and is unavailable in some countries e.g. the UK.  |
| Provides archived audio for many airports and frequencies.                             | Many jurisdictions prohibit streaming ATC communications for legal/regulatory reasons.              |
| Covers multiple ATC frequencies including Tower, Ground, Approach, Departure and ATIS. | Not all airports offer archived recordings.                                                         |
| Enables investigators to reconstruct aviation events using real-time communications.   | Audio quality can vary depending on receiver location and radio conditions.                         |
| Complements flight tracking platforms by providing operational context.                | Communications may be incomplete if multiple frequencies are involved.                              |

### Summary

LiveATC is a valuable verification and contextualisation tool within the OSINT workflow. Combined with ADS-B tracking platforms, airport data and official aviation records, it helps investigators understand operational decisions, emergency responses and the sequence of events surrounding an aircraft movement.

### Ownership

Owned and operated by its founder, [Dave Pascoe](https://www.linkedin.com/in/davepascoe), who launched the air traffic control audio network in 2003. Dave is based in New Hampshire, USA.

### Ethical Considerations

* Respect local laws governing the monitoring and redistribution of air traffic communications.
* Do not interfere with aviation communications or attempt to transmit on aviation frequencies.
* Verify significant findings using official aviation authorities and investigation reports.
* Consider operational sensitivity when reporting live aviation incidents.
* Avoid publishing speculative interpretations of communications without corroborating evidence.
* Be aware that some audio may contain personal names or operationally sensitive information.
